# Finished Goods Inventory

Harvested products become finished inventory.

Examples:

- Grade A Eggplant
- Grade B Tomato
- Lettuce Pack 250g

---

## Inventory Tracking

Tracks:

- Quantity Available
- Reserved Quantity
- Sold Quantity
- Expired Quantity

---

## FIFO Selling

The oldest available batch is sold first.

This prevents:

- Spoilage
- Incorrect costing

---

Reserved
