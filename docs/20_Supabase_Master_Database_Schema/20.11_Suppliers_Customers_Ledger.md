# Suppliers and Customers Ledger

The ERP maintains a unified business partner database.

Partners can be:

- Supplier
- Customer
- Both Supplier and Customer

Examples:

Supplier:
East-West Seed Corporation

Customer:
Resort A

Both:
A farm cooperative that buys and sells products.

---

## Main Information

Each partner contains:

- Partner ID
- Business Name
- Contact Person
- Address
- Mobile Number
- Email
- Tax Information
- Payment Terms
- Credit Limit
- Status

---

## Financial Integration

Customers connect to:

- Sales
- Invoices
- Accounts Receivable
- Payment History

Suppliers connect to:

- Purchases
- Receiving
- Accounts Payable
- Payment History

---

## Complete Ledger History

Every partner has a ledger showing:

Date | Transaction | Debit | Credit | Balance
