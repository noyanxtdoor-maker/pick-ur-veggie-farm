# Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable

The ERP tracks money owed by customers and money owed to suppliers.

---

## Accounts Receivable

Customer credit transactions create receivables.

Example:

Resort A buys vegetables worth ₱15,000.

Invoice:
+ ₱15,000 Receivable

Payment:
- ₱15,000 Receivable

Remaining Balance:
₱0

---

## Accounts Payable

Supplier purchases on credit create payables.

Example:

Buy fertilizer:

Invoice:
₱25,000 Payable

Payment:
Reduces payable balance.

---

## Features

- Customer credit limits
- Payment terms
- Due dates
- Aging reports
- Payment history
- Overdue alerts

---

## Integration

Customer and supplier records link directly with:

- Sales
- Purchasing
- Accounting
