# Sales Orders and Invoices

The system supports multiple sales models.

## Retail Sales

Immediate payment.

Inventory is deducted instantly.

---

## Wholesale Sales

Allows:

- Customer accounts
- Credit terms
- Partial payments

---

## Pre-Orders

A customer reserves future production.

Inventory is not deducted until actual fulfillment.

---

## Sales Documents

Sales Order
↓
Delivery
↓
Invoice
↓
Payment

Financial status and delivery status are tracked separately.
