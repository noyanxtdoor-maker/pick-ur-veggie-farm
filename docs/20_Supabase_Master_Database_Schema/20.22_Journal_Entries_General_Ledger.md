# Journal Entries and General Ledger

The General Ledger is the financial brain of the ERP.

Every operational action can generate accounting entries.

---

## Purchase Example

Buy fertilizer worth ₱10,000:

Debit:
Fertilizer Inventory ₱10,000

Credit:
Cash or Accounts Payable ₱10,000

---

## Consumption Example

Use fertilizer for Eggplant Tunnel A:

Debit:
Crop Production Cost ₱500

Credit:
Fertilizer Inventory ₱500

---

## Harvest Example

Harvest converts biological assets into finished goods inventory.

Debit:
Finished Goods Inventory

Credit:
Biological Asset

---

## Sale Example

Sell 50 kg of eggplant:

Entry 1:
Debit:
Cash / Accounts Receivable

Credit:
Sales Revenue

Entry 2:
Debit:
Cost of Goods Sold

Credit:
Finished Goods Inventory

---

## Integrity Rule

Journal entries cannot be deleted.

Corrections are made using reversal or adjustment entries.

Complete audit history is maintained.
