# Purchase and Receiving System

The purchase workflow controls the movement of materials into inventory.

Workflow:

Purchase Request
↓
Purchase Approval
↓
Purchase Order
↓
Supplier Delivery
↓
Receiving Inspection
↓
Inventory Batch Creation
↓
Accounting Entry

---

## Receiving Information

The system records:

- Supplier
- Purchase Order Number
- Date Received
- Item
- Quantity
- Unit Cost
- Batch Number
- Expiration Date
- Receiver

---

## Financial Effect

Example:

Buy 10 bags of fertilizer.

Inventory Asset increases.

Cash decreases or Accounts Payable increases.

---

## Quality Control

Received items may be:

- Accepted
- Partially Accepted
- Rejected

Rejected items create supplier claims.
