# Personal Workspace Calendar

Every user has a private calendar.

Examples:

- Family events
- Personal appointments
- Personal reminders

---

## Privacy Rules

Private events are invisible to:

- Owners
- Administrators
- Operators
- Developers
- Other employees

unless explicitly shared.

---

## Visibility Levels

Level 1:
Private

Level 2:
Shared with selected users

Level 3:
Team Calendar

Level 4:
Company Public

Level 5:
Executive Confidential

---

## Separation Principle

Company events update ERP workflows.

Personal events have no impact on company records.
