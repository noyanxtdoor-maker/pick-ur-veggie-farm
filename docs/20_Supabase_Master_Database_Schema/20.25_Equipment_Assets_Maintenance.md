# Equipment Assets and Maintenance

Tracks farm assets and their operating costs.

Examples:

- Pumps
- Generators
- Vehicles
- Tools
- Sprayers
- Solar Systems

---

## Asset Information

Stores:

- Asset ID
- Purchase Date
- Cost
- Warranty
- Location
- Current Condition

---

## Maintenance Records

Tracks:

- Scheduled maintenance
- Repairs
- Spare parts used
- Labor cost
- Downtime

---

## Replacement Intelligence

The ERP analyzes:

Total repair cost

versus

Replacement value

and warns when replacement may be more economical.
