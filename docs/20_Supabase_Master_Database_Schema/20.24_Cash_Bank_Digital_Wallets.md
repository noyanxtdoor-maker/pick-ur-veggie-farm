# Cash, Bank and Digital Wallet Management

The ERP manages all money storage locations.

Examples:

- Cash Drawer
- Petty Cash
- Bank Accounts
- GCash
- Maya

---

## Daily Cash Control

Tracks:

Opening Balance

+ Cash Sales

+ Cash Deposits

- Expenses

- Withdrawals

= Closing Balance

---

## Bank Reconciliation

The system compares:

ERP records

versus

Bank statements

to identify missing or incorrect transactions.

---

## Transfer Management

Example:

Transfer ₱10,000 from GCash to Bank.

Debit:
Bank Account

Credit:
GCash

No expense is created.
