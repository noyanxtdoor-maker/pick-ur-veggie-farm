# Delivery and Logistics

Controls the movement of products to customers.

---

## Delivery Records

Stores:

- Delivery Number
- Customer
- Driver
- Vehicle
- Route
- Delivery Date
- Delivery Expense
- Status

---

## Delivery Status

Pending

In Transit

Delivered

## Accounting Relationship

Delivery costs can be assigned to:

- Operating Expense

or

- Specific Customer Order

for profitability analysis.
