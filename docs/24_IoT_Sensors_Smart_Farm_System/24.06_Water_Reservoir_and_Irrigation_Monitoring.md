# Water Reservoir and Irrigation Monitoring

## Purpose

Water is the foundation of crop production.

The system monitors water availability, usage, and irrigation performance.

---

## Water Monitoring Devices

Supported devices:

- Tank level sensors
- Flow meters
- Pressure sensors
- Water temperature sensors

---

## Reservoir Monitoring

Examples:

Main Nutrient Tank

Capacity:
1,000 liters

Current Level:
200 liters

Codex Alert:

"Reservoir level is below 20%. Prepare refill."

---

## Water Consumption Analysis

The system tracks:

- Daily usage
- Weekly trends
- Seasonal demand
- Crop water requirements

---

## Leak Detection

Example:

Normal daily consumption:

500 liters

Current day:

900 liters

Codex warning:

"Water usage increased by 80%. Check for leaks or abnormal consumption."

---

## Pressure Monitoring

Low pressure may indicate:

- Clogged drippers
- Pipe leaks
- Pump problems

---

## Irrigation Performance History

The ERP stores:

- Water consumption per crop
- Water consumption per production zone
- Irrigation efficiency trends

---

## Integration with Production

Codex compares water usage with:

- Plant age
- Growth stage
- Weather conditions
- Harvest results

---

## Principle

Water should be measured and optimized like any valuable farm resource.
