# IoT Stress Test Scenarios

## Purpose

Before deployment, the IoT infrastructure must survive real-world farm conditions.

---

## Scenario 1: Internet Failure During Operation

Problem:

The farm loses internet for 12 hours.

Expected Behavior:

- Sensors continue collecting data
- ESP32 stores readings locally
- Data automatically synchronizes after connection returns

---

## Scenario 2: EC Sensor Failure

Problem:

EC sensor reports impossible values.

Expected Behavior:

- Codex detects abnormal readings
- Sensor is marked for inspection
- Alerts are sent to responsible users

---

## Scenario 3: Water Tank Suddenly Drops

Problem:

Tank level drops from 80% to 10% within 10 minutes.

Expected Behavior:

Codex warns:

"Unusual water loss detected."

Possible causes:

- Major leak
- Pipe failure
- Sensor malfunction

---

## Scenario 4: Power Failure

Problem:

The farm experiences a blackout.

Expected Behavior:

- Devices reconnect after power returns
- Missing periods are recorded
- System reports downtime

---

## Scenario 5: Unauthorized Device Attempt

Problem:

An unknown device tries to send data.

Expected Behavior:

- Connection is rejected
- Security event is logged
- Administrators receive notification

---

## Scenario 6: Extreme Environmental Conditions

Problem:

Greenhouse temperature reaches 45°C.

Expected Behavior:

- Critical alert generated
- Codex recommends emergency actions
- Event becomes part of crop history

---

## Scenario 7: Incorrect Sensor Calibration

Problem:

A pH sensor drifts from its true value.

Expected Behavior:

- Calibration reminders are triggered
- Historical inconsistencies are detected
- Maintenance is scheduled

---

## Final IoT Principle

The purpose of IoT is not to add gadgets.

The purpose is to give farmers reliable information at the right time.

Technology must remain affordable, maintainable, and practical for real agricultural conditions.
