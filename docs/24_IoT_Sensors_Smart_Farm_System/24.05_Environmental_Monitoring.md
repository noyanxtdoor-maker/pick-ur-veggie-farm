# Environmental Monitoring

## Purpose

Environmental conditions strongly affect crop growth, health, and productivity.

Continuous monitoring allows problems to be identified before they cause major losses.

---

## Environmental Sensors

Supported sensors:

- Air temperature
- Relative humidity
- Light intensity
- Rain detection
- Wind speed (future)
- CO₂ concentration (future)

---

## Temperature Monitoring

Examples:

Lettuce Nursery

Normal Range:
18–28°C

Alert:
Above 32°C

Codex notification:

"Nursery temperature is higher than recommended. Inspect shading and ventilation."

---

## Humidity Monitoring

Codex monitors:

- High humidity disease risk
- Low humidity plant stress
- Sudden environmental changes

Example:

"Humidity remained above 90% for 8 hours. Disease monitoring is recommended."

---

## Light Monitoring

Useful for:

- Seedling nurseries
- Greenhouses
- Shade management

Codex can analyze:

- Daily light exposure
- Light shortages
- Excessive sunlight

---

## Environmental History

The ERP creates:

- Hourly trends
- Daily averages
- Monthly comparisons
- Seasonal reports

---

## Production Correlation

Codex can compare environmental data with:

- Yield
- Crop quality
- Disease occurrence
- Growth speed

---

## Principle

The farm should not rely only on what workers observe.

The environment should continuously report its condition.
