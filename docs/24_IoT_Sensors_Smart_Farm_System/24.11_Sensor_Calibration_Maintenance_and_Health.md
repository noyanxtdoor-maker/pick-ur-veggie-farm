# Sensor Calibration, Maintenance and Health

## Purpose

Sensor accuracy determines the quality of decisions.

Incorrect sensor data can be more dangerous than having no sensor data.

---

## Sensor Maintenance Records

The ERP tracks:

- Installation date
- Calibration schedule
- Last calibration date
- Calibration results
- Cleaning history
- Repairs
- Replacement history

---

## Calibration Alerts

Examples:

"EC Sensor #3 requires calibration in 3 days."

"pH Sensor #2 has exceeded its calibration interval."

---

## Sensor Health Monitoring

The ERP detects:

- Missing data
- Unusual readings
- Constant values that never change
- Communication failures
- Low battery levels

---

## Example

Temperature sensor:

Normal:

28°C to 35°C variation daily

Current:

30°C for 48 hours continuously

Codex Alert:

"Sensor may be malfunctioning. Verify device operation."

---

## Maintenance Integration

Sensor maintenance connects with:

- Equipment management
- Technician assignments
- Maintenance schedules
- Spare parts inventory

---

## Accuracy Principle

Better decisions require trustworthy data.

A smart farm starts with reliable sensors.
