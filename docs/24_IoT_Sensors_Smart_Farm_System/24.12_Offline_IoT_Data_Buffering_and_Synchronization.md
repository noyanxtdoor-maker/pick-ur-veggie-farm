# Offline IoT Data Buffering and Synchronization

## Purpose

Island farms may experience unstable internet.

IoT devices must continue collecting information even when cloud services are unavailable.

---

## Offline Data Buffer

ESP32 devices or IoT gateways temporarily store:

- Sensor readings
- Timestamps
- Device status
- Error logs

---

## Synchronization Workflow

Normal Operation:

Sensor

↓

ESP32

↓

Cloud Database

↓

ERP

---

Internet Failure:

Sensor

↓

ESP32 Storage

↓

Wait for Connection

↓

Automatic Synchronization

↓

Cloud Database

---

## Data Integrity

Synchronization prevents:

- Duplicate records
- Missing readings
- Incorrect timestamps

---

## Conflict Handling

If conflicting data exists:

- Original records remain stored
- Conflicts are flagged
- Administrators review unusual situations

---

## Reliability Principle

The farm should continue operating even when the internet stops.

Connectivity problems should never result in lost farm data.
