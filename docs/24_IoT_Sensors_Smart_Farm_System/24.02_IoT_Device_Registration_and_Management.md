# IoT Device Registration and Management

## Purpose

A smart farm may contain dozens or hundreds of sensors.

The ERP must know what each device is, where it is installed, and whether it is functioning properly.

---

## Device Master Record

Each device receives a digital profile.

Examples:

Device ID:

IOT-TEMP-001

Name:

Greenhouse Temperature Sensor 1

---

## Device Information

The system records:

- Device type
- Manufacturer
- Model
- Serial number
- Installation date
- Farm location
- Production zone
- Assigned equipment
- Responsible technician

---

## Device Status

Examples:

🟢 Online

Sensor is sending data normally.

---

🟡 Warning

Battery low or irregular readings.

---

🔴 Offline

No communication detected.

---

## Device Lifecycle

Purchase

↓

Installation

↓

Calibration

↓

Operation

↓

Maintenance

↓

Replacement

---

## Maintenance History

The ERP tracks:

- Cleaning dates
- Calibration dates
- Repairs
- Component replacements
- Failure history

---

## Device Inventory Integration

IoT devices are also farm assets.

Therefore, they connect with:

- Equipment Management
- Maintenance Records
- Accounting Asset Records

---

## Scalability Principle

The architecture should support:

- One sensor in a small farm
- Hundreds of devices in a commercial operation

without redesigning the system.
