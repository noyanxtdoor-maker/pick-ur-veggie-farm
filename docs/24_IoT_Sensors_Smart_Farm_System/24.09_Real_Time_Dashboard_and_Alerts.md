# Real-Time IoT Dashboard and Alerts

## Purpose

The IoT dashboard converts thousands of sensor readings into simple and actionable farm intelligence.

Farm owners and workers should understand the farm condition at a glance.

---

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard displays:

- Current farm status
- Active alerts
- Environmental conditions
- Water system status
- Nutrient conditions
- Equipment health
- Energy status

---

## Alert Levels

### Information

Routine notifications.

Examples:

- Daily summaries
- Scheduled maintenance reminders

---

### Warning

Requires attention.

Examples:

- Low water level
- Rising temperature
- Increasing pump runtime

---

### Critical

Immediate action required.

Examples:

- Pump failure
- Extreme temperatures
- Reservoir empty
- Sensor failure affecting operations

---

## Alert Delivery

Alerts may be sent through:

- ERP dashboard
- Mobile notifications
- Email
- SMS
- Future messaging integrations

---

## Alert History

Every alert records:

- Date and time
- Sensor involved
- Location
- Severity
- Response actions
- Person who resolved the issue

---

## Principle

A smart dashboard does not display more information.

It displays the right information at the right time.
