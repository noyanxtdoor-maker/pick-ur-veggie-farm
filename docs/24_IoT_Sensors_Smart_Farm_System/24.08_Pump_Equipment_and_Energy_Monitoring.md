# Pump, Equipment and Energy Monitoring

## Purpose

Equipment failures can destroy crops quickly.

Continuous monitoring allows early warning and preventive maintenance.

---

## Monitored Equipment

Examples:

- Irrigation pumps
- Aeration pumps
- Solar systems
- Batteries
- Generators
- Water filtration systems

---

## Pump Monitoring

The system tracks:

- Runtime hours
- Operating schedules
- Abnormal running time
- Power consumption

---

## Example

Normal:

Pump operates 2 hours/day

Current:

Pump operates 6 hours/day

Codex Alert:

"Pump runtime increased significantly. Inspect for leaks or system problems."

---

## Solar Monitoring

The ERP can monitor:

- Solar production
- Battery charge level
- Energy consumption
- System efficiency

---

## Preventive Maintenance

Codex can recommend:

- Cleaning schedules
- Equipment inspection
- Component replacement

Example:

"Pump #2 has operated 1,000 hours since its last service."

---

## Equipment Failure Prediction

Using historical data, Codex can identify:

- Declining efficiency
- Increasing energy consumption
- Irregular operating patterns

---

## Integration with Existing ERP Modules

IoT equipment data connects to:

- Equipment Asset Management
- Maintenance Records
- Accounting depreciation
- Production cost analysis

---

## Principle

The best equipment repair is the one that happens before a failure.
