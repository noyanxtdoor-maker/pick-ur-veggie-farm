# IoT Philosophy and Smart Farm Architecture

## Purpose

The purpose of IoT is to give the farm digital senses.

Instead of depending only on human observation, the farm continuously measures important conditions.

## Sensor Categories

The ERP supports several sensor groups.

### Environmental Sensors

Monitor:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Light intensity
- Rain conditions

---

### Water Sensors

Monitor:

- Tank level
- Water flow
- Water temperature
- Pressure

---

### Nutrient Sensors

Monitor:

- Electrical conductivity (EC)
- pH

---

### Equipment Sensors

Monitor:

- Pump runtime
- Electricity usage
- Solar system performance

---

## V1 Monitoring Approach

Example:

Temperature:

Sensor Reading:

39°C

Codex Alert:

"Greenhouse temperature is unusually high. Inspect ventilation and shading."

The ERP does not automatically activate equipment in Version 1.

## Farm Reality Principle

A simple reliable system is better than a complicated system that frequently fails.

PickUrVeggie V3 prioritizes reliability, affordability, and ease of maintenance.
