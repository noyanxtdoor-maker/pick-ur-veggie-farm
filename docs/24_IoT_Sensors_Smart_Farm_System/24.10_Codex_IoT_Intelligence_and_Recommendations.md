# Codex IoT Intelligence and Recommendations

## Purpose

Codex transforms raw sensor data into understandable recommendations.

Sensors provide measurements.

Codex provides intelligence.

---

## Example 1: Temperature Stress

Sensor:

Temperature:
39°C

Codex:

"Tunnel A temperature is above normal.

Possible risks:

- Plant stress
- Reduced pollination
- Faster water loss

Recommended actions:

- Check shade systems
- Increase ventilation
- Monitor crop condition"

---

## Example 2: Nutrient Issue

Sensor:

EC:
0.7

Target:
1.5

Codex:

"EC is significantly below target.

Review nutrient preparation and recent water additions."

---

## Example 3: Equipment Problem

Sensor:

Pump runtime increased from:

2 hours/day

to:

6 hours/day

Codex:

"Unusual pump activity detected.

Possible causes:

- Leakage
- Clogged system
- Pump inefficiency"

---

## Multi-Data Analysis

The power of Codex comes from combining:

- Sensor history
- Crop stage
- Weather
- Production records
- Inventory
- Equipment history

---

Example:

"Tomato yield decreased during a period of high temperature and reduced nutrient stability."

---

## Recommendation Philosophy

Codex may:

- Analyze
- Predict
- Warn
- Recommend

Codex does not automatically change equipment in Version 1.

---

## Human Responsibility Principle

Technology supports farmers.

Final farm decisions remain with people.
