# Mobile Camera, Photo and Document Capture

## Purpose

The smartphone camera becomes a farm documentation tool.

Photos provide evidence, improve communication, and support AI analysis.

---

# Supported Uses

Crop Monitoring:

- Pest damage
- Disease symptoms
- Nutrient deficiency
- Growth progress

---

Harvest Records:

- Product quality
- Damage reports
- Harvest evidence

---

Equipment:

- Machine failures
- Broken parts
- Maintenance documentation

---

Documents:

- Purchase receipts
- Supplier invoices
- Delivery documents

---

# Camera Workflow

Capture Photo

↓

Compress Image

↓

Attach to ERP Record

↓

Store in Upload Queue

↓

Synchronize when internet returns

---

# Image Optimization

The mobile app automatically manages:

- Image compression
- File size limits
- Upload retries
- Storage cleanup after successful synchronization

---

# Codex AI Integration

Users may ask:

"Codex, what is wrong with this leaf?"

The system can use AI image analysis for:

- Pest identification
- Disease detection
- Nutrient problem analysis

---

# Privacy Principle

The application only accesses photos selected or captured for ERP activities.

It does not access the user's personal photo library without permission.
