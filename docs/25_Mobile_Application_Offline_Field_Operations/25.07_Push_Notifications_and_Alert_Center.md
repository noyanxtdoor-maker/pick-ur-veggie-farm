# Push Notifications and Alert Center

## Purpose

The mobile application keeps users informed about important activities without requiring them to constantly open the ERP.

---

# Notification Categories

Information:

Examples:

- Today's assignments
- Upcoming schedules
- Training reminders

---

Warning:

Examples:

- Overdue tasks
- Low inventory alerts
- Missing attendance records

---

Critical:

Examples:

- Equipment failures
- Water shortages
- Severe crop problems

---

# Role-Based Notifications

Farm Worker receives:

- Personal assignments
- Task reminders
- Attendance notices

---

Supervisor receives:

- Team issues
- Production alerts
- Approval requests

---

Owner receives:

- Critical farm events
- Financial warnings
- Codex executive summaries

---

# Notification Controls

Users can configure:

- Notification preferences
- Silent hours
- Alert categories

---

# Alert History

The ERP records:

- Notification sent
- Recipient
- Date and time
- Read status
- User response

---

# Notification Principle

A useful alert prevents a problem.

Too many alerts become noise.
