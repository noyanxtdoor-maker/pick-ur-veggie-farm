# System Architecture Overview

## Purpose

This file provides a high-level blueprint of how all PickUrVeggie ERP V3 packages connect and operate as one integrated system.

The ERP is not a collection of separate applications.

It is a single connected ecosystem where every action, record, and decision flows through a controlled and auditable process.

---

# Overall System Philosophy

The architecture follows this principle:

Physical Farm Activity

↓

Data Capture

↓

ERP Database

↓

Business Logic

↓

Financial Impact

↓

Analytics & Codex Intelligence

↓

Human Decision

---

## Example Workflow

A worker applies fertilizer.

↓

Mobile application records the activity.

↓

Inventory quantity decreases.

↓

Production cost increases.

↓

↓

Reports are updated.

↓

## System Integration Rules

Every major operation must have:

### 1. Source

Who or what initiated the action.

Examples:

- Employee
- Sensor
- Manager
- AI draft

---

### 2. Record

A permanent database transaction.

---

### 3. Permission Validation

The user or device must have authority to perform the action.

---

### 4. Audit Trail

The system records:

- Who performed the action
- Date and time
- Device used
- Changes made

---

### 5. Financial Impact

If the activity affects money or assets, the accounting system must recognize it.

## Golden Architecture Rules

Rule 1:

A physical event must happen before a financial event.

Example:

A fertilizer application occurs first.

Accounting records the cost afterward.

---

Rule 2:

AI provides recommendations but does not control critical business decisions.

Codex cannot:

- Approve payroll
- Move money
- Delete records
- Override permissions

---

Rule 3:

Nothing is permanently deleted.

The ERP uses:

- Archive
- Void
- Reversal entries
- Audit history

---

Rule 4:

The ERP must continue operating even without internet.

Offline records are synchronized once connection returns.

---

Rule 5:

Every piece of data belongs to a company and follows permissions.

No user should see information outside their authorized access.

---

# Final Architecture Principle

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 is designed as a digital twin of the farm.

Every plant, worker, inventory item, equipment, transaction, and business event should have a reliable digital history.

The goal is not simply to store data.

The goal is to transform farm activities into knowledge, intelligence, accountability, and better decisions.
