# Financial Posting Rules

## Purpose

Financial records must always represent real business events.

Accounting does not create reality.

Accounting records what actually happened.

---

# Core Accounting Flow

Real World Event

↓

Operational Record

↓

Validation

↓

Accounting Transaction

↓

Financial Report

---

# Source Document Rule

Every financial transaction must have a supporting source.

Examples:

Purchase:
Purchase Order → Receiving → Invoice → Accounting Entry

Sales:
Sales Order → Delivery → Invoice → Revenue Recognition

Payroll:
Attendance → Payroll Calculation → Approval → Expense Recording

Inventory:
Consumption → Production Cost → Accounting Impact

---

# Double Entry Integrity

Every accounting transaction must balance.

Example:

Purchase fertilizer:

Inventory Asset + ₱5,000

Accounts Payable + ₱5,000

---

# Inventory Financial Rules

Inventory movements automatically affect financial records when applicable.

Examples:

Purchase:

Inventory increases.

Liability or cash increases.

---

Consumption:

Inventory decreases.

Production expense increases.

---

Waste:

Inventory decreases.

Loss or waste expense increases.

---

# Sales Financial Rules

A completed sale may generate:

- Revenue
- Accounts Receivable or Cash
- Cost of Goods Sold
- Inventory reduction

---

# Payroll Rules

Payroll creates:

- Salary expense
- Employee payable or cash reduction

---

# Period Closing Rule

After an accounting period is closed:

Not allowed:

- Editing old transactions
- Deleting entries
- Changing balances

Corrections must use:

- Adjustments
- Reversals
- New audit records

---

# Manual Adjustments

Only authorized users can create financial adjustments.

Requirements:

- Proper permission
- Reason for adjustment
- Approval workflow
- Audit history

---

# Financial Integrity Principle

Money records must tell the complete story.

Mistakes are corrected by adding history, never by erasing history.
