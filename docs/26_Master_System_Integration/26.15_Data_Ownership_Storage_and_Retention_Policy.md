# Data Ownership, Storage and Retention Policy

## Purpose

This document defines who owns data, how long it is stored, and how storage costs are controlled.

---

# Data Ownership

Company operational data belongs to the company.

Examples:

- Crop history
- Inventory records
- Sales history
- Financial records
- Employee work history
- Equipment history

Users own their personal preferences:

- Language settings
- Notification preferences
- Personal notes

---

# Data Retention Policy

## Permanent Records

Keep indefinitely:

- Accounting records
- Inventory history
- Sales history
- Purchase history
- Employee history
- Payroll records
- Audit logs

---

## Operational Media

Examples:

- Daily crop photos
- Pest images
- Temporary evidence

Policy:

- Compress before upload.
- Keep based on company settings.
- Default recommendation: 90 days.

---

## AI Related Data

Keep:

- AI requests.
- AI responses.
- Important conclusions.

Original files may be deleted according to storage policy.

---

# Storage Optimization

The system should:

- Compress images.
- Reduce unnecessary files.
- Archive old data.
- Allow export to external storage.

---

# Final Data Principle

Data is a company asset.

It must be protected, controlled, and managed responsibly.
