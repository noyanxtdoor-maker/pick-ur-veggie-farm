# Codex AI Authority Matrix

## Purpose

Codex AI is the intelligent assistant of PickUrVeggie ERP V3.

Codex assists humans but never replaces business authority.

---

# Codex Can Do

Analysis:

- Analyze crop performance
- Detect trends
- Compare historical data
- Forecast possible outcomes

Knowledge:

- Explain SOPs
- Provide agricultural information
- Answer user questions

Assistance:

- Create drafts
- Prepare reports
- Suggest schedules
- Recommend actions

---

# Codex Cannot Do

Financial:

- Send money
- Approve payments
- Approve payroll
- Change accounting records

Security:

- Create users
- Change permissions
- Disable security controls

Data:

- Delete records
- Remove audit logs
- Modify historical records

---

# AI Confidence Rule

Codex must identify information sources:

ERP Facts:

Data directly retrieved from PickUrVeggie records.

AI Knowledge:

General knowledge and recommendations.

Predictions:

Forecasts based on historical patterns.

---

# Human Approval Levels

Level 1:
Information only.

Example:
"Show last month's lettuce production."

---

Level 2:
Recommendation.

Example:
"Suggest a fertilizer schedule."

Human decides.

---

Level 3:
Draft automation.

Example:
Create a task reminder.

Human may approve.

---

# AI Failure Policy

If AI services are unavailable:

- Farm operations continue
- ERP functions remain available
- AI features are temporarily disabled

---

# Final AI Principle

Codex is an advisor.

The farmer, manager, and company leadership always make final decisions.
