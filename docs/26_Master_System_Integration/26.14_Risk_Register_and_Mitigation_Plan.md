# Risk Register and Mitigation Plan

## Purpose

Every enterprise system contains risks.

A professional ERP does not assume problems will never happen.

It identifies risks, reduces their probability, and prepares recovery plans.

---

# Risk 1: Data Duplication

Risk:

The same information is stored in multiple places and becomes inconsistent.

Mitigation:

- Single source of truth.
- Master entity relationships.
- UUID-based records.
- Controlled references.

---

# Risk 2: Offline Synchronization Conflicts

Risk:

Multiple users modify related records while offline.

Mitigation:

- Conflict detection.
- No silent overwrites.
- Human review workflow.
- Complete audit history.

---

# Risk 3: Unauthorized Access

Risk:

Users access information beyond their authority.

Mitigation:

- Role-based permissions.
- Device trust.
- Authentication controls.
- Audit logs.

---

# Risk 4: Financial Corruption

Risk:

Money records are changed without proper evidence.

Mitigation:

- Source document requirements.
- Double-entry accounting.
- Locked accounting periods.
- Adjustment and reversal entries.

---

# Risk 5: AI Overreach

Risk:

AI makes decisions it should not control.

Mitigation:

- AI authority boundaries.
- Human approval requirements.
- Source classification.
- AI activity records.

---

# Risk 6: Device Theft or Loss

Risk:

A phone containing company data is lost.

Mitigation:

- Device revocation.
- Encrypted storage.
- Session expiration.
- Minimal offline data.

---

# Risk 7: Cloud Provider Failure

Risk:

Supabase becomes unavailable.

Mitigation:

- Offline operation.
- Backup strategy.
- Data export capability.
- Recovery procedures.

---

# Risk 8: Storage Limits

Risk:

The free cloud storage plan becomes full.

Mitigation:

- Image compression.
- Retention policies.
- Automatic cleanup.
- External storage options.

---

# Risk 9: Human Error

Risk:

Workers enter incorrect information.

Mitigation:

- Validation rules.
- Approval workflows.
- Training.
- Audit history.

---

# Final Risk Principle

Risk cannot be eliminated completely.

The objective is to make failures:

- Detectable
- Containable
- Recoverable
- Auditable
