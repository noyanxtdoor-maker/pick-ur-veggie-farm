# AI Privacy, Data Ownership and Confidentiality

## Purpose

PickUrVeggie ERP stores valuable and sensitive business information.

AI integration must never compromise ownership of company data.

---

## Sensitive Information Includes

Business:

- Revenue
- Profit margins
- Customer lists
- Supplier pricing
- Financial records

Operations:

- Production methods
- Crop performance
- Inventory movement
- Yield history

Intellectual Property:

- Gomez Method
- DFG system
- Internal SOPs
- Proprietary research

---

## Data Ownership Principle

All farm data belongs to the company using the ERP.

AI providers are tools for analysis.

They are not owners of farm information.

---

## Data Sharing Control

Companies can configure:

- Which AI services are allowed
- What information can be shared
- Which users can use external AI features

---

## Confidentiality Levels

Public Information:

General agricultural knowledge.

Internal Information:

Farm procedures and SOPs.

Confidential Information:

Financial data, customer data, and proprietary methods.

---

## AI Request Filtering

Before data leaves the ERP:

The system should evaluate:

- Is external AI required?
- Is sensitive information being shared?
- Does the user have permission?

---

## Security Principle

AI should make the farm smarter.

It should never expose the farm's secrets.
