# AI Inventory and Supply Intelligence

## Purpose

Codex transforms inventory management from a reactive process into a predictive system.

Instead of saying:

"You have no fertilizer left."

Codex says:

"You will run out of fertilizer next week."

---

## Inventory Prediction

Codex analyzes:

- Current stock
- Historical usage
- Seasonal demand
- Crop production schedules
- Delivery lead times

---

## Smart Reorder Recommendations

Example:

Item:

Calcium Nitrate


Current Stock:

30 kg


Average Daily Use:

5 kg/day


Days Remaining:

6 days


Supplier Delivery Time:

5 days


Codex Recommendation:

"Order Calcium Nitrate today to avoid production interruption."

---

## Dynamic Reorder Points

Instead of fixed minimum stock levels, Codex considers:

- Current production scale
- Upcoming crop schedules
- Historical consumption
- Supplier reliability
- Delivery delays

---

## Supplier Intelligence

Codex analyzes suppliers based on:

- Price trends
- Delivery speed
- Product quality history
- Purchase frequency
- Reliability score

Example:

"Supplier A is 10% cheaper but has frequent late deliveries."

---

## Inventory Waste Analysis

Codex identifies:

- Expired materials
- Slow-moving items
- Frequent spoilage
- Excess purchases

---

## Purchase Recommendations

Codex can prepare:

- Purchase requests
- Suggested quantities
- Preferred suppliers

However:

Final approval remains with authorized users.

---

## Fraud and Error Detection

Examples:

- Unusual stock adjustments
- Excessive material usage
- Repeated inventory losses
- Suspicious consumption patterns

---

## Strategic Benefit

Inventory becomes an intelligent planning system rather than simply a stock counter.
