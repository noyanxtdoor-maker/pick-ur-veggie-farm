# AI Harvest Yield and Demand Forecasting

## Purpose

Codex helps management plan future production, sales, and supply requirements.

The goal is to match production with market demand.

---

## Harvest Forecasting

Codex estimates:

- Expected harvest dates
- Expected quantities
- Harvest duration
- Production peaks

Example:

"Eggplant Tunnel A is projected to produce 300 kg during the next 14 days."

---

## Yield Prediction Factors

Codex considers:

- Crop variety
- Plant age
- Historical yield
- Weather patterns
- Pest history
- Nutrient records
- Environmental conditions

---

## Sales Demand Forecasting

Codex analyzes:

- Historical sales
- Customer purchasing patterns
- Seasonal demand
- Tourism activity
- Special events

Example:

"Vegetable demand is expected to increase 20% during the holiday season."

---

## Production Planning

Codex may recommend:

- Increasing planting volume
- Reducing production
- Adjusting planting schedules
- Reserving inventory

---

## Customer Intelligence

Codex identifies:

- Most valuable customers
- Purchasing trends
- Declining customer activity
- New sales opportunities

---

## Financial Forecasting Integration

Codex can estimate:

- Future revenue
- Expected production cost
- Expected profit margin
- Future cash requirements

---

## Risk Forecasting

Examples:

"Expected harvest volume may not satisfy confirmed pre-orders."

or:

"Projected production exceeds expected demand."

---

## Strategic Planning

The owner can ask:

"Codex, what should I plant next month to maximize profit?"

Codex evaluates:

- Market demand
- Historical performance
- Production capacity
- Available resources

and provides recommendations.

---

## Final Principle

A smart farm does not only record the past.

It uses data to prepare for the future.
