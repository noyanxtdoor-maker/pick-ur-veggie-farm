# Multi-AI Gateway Architecture

## Purpose

Different AI systems have different strengths.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 should not depend on a single AI provider.

The AI Gateway allows Codex to select the best intelligence source.

---

## Architecture

User

↓

🐾 Codex Farm Companion

↓

AI Gateway

↓

Local ERP Intelligence
Google Gemini
OpenAI
Future AI Providers

---

## Local ERP Intelligence

Primary intelligence engine.

Used for:

- Inventory warnings
- Calendar reminders
- Basic calculations
- KPI monitoring
- Business rules

Cost:

₱0

---

## Google Gemini

Best suited for:

- Image understanding
- Pest and disease analysis
- Document reading
- Agricultural knowledge

---

## OpenAI

Best suited for:

- Conversation
- Executive summaries
- Financial explanations
- Complex business analysis

---

## AI Selection Principle

The system should always attempt to use:

1. Local intelligence first
2. Lowest cost AI suitable for the task
3. Advanced AI only when necessary

---

## Vendor Independence

Future AI providers can be added without changing the Codex experience.

The user always communicates with Codex.

The technology behind Codex may evolve over time.
