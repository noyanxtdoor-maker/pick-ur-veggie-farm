# AI Crop Production and Fertigation Advisor

## Purpose

Codex acts as a digital farm advisor by combining:

- Crop records
- Production history
- Fertigation data
- Environmental information
- Agricultural knowledge

to provide practical recommendations.

---

## Crop Monitoring

Codex tracks:

- Plant age
- Growth stage
- Flowering stage
- Fruiting stage
- Harvest stage
- Historical performance

---

## Fertigation Intelligence

Codex analyzes:

- EC trends
- pH trends
- Nutrient consumption
- Fertigation frequency
- Water usage

Examples:

"EC has been decreasing for 5 days while fruit load is increasing."

Possible recommendation:

"Review nutrient concentration and increase monitoring."

---

## Crop-Specific Recommendations

Example:

DFG Eggplant:

Current Stage:

Heavy fruiting


Codex Suggests:

- Monitor potassium demand
- Increase harvest frequency
- Check plant support systems

---

## Production Optimization

Codex helps identify:

- High-performing crop blocks
- Low-performing areas
- Best varieties
- Best planting schedules

---

## Disease Prevention Intelligence

Using historical records, Codex can identify:

Examples:

"During previous rainy seasons, powdery mildew increased by 40%."

Recommendation:

"Increase monitoring before high-risk periods."

---

## Resource Efficiency Analysis

Codex calculates:

- Yield per plant
- Yield per square meter
- Fertilizer efficiency
- Water efficiency
- Labor efficiency

---

## Human Decision Rule

Codex provides recommendations.

Farm managers decide whether to implement them.

AI never directly changes crop management settings.
