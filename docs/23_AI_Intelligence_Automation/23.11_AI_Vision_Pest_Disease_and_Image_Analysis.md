# AI Vision Pest, Disease and Image Analysis

## Purpose

Codex can use AI vision services to analyze farm images and assist with crop health monitoring.

---

## Supported Image Analysis

Examples:

- Pest identification
- Disease symptom analysis
- Nutrient deficiency recognition
- Leaf discoloration analysis
- Fruit quality inspection
- Growth comparison over time
- Physical damage assessment

---

## Worker Workflow

Worker notices a problem.

↓

Open Codex.

↓

Capture crop photo.

↓

Select crop and location.

↓

AI analyzes the image.

↓

Codex provides possible explanations and recommendations.

---

## Example Response

"Possible issue detected:

Crop:
Eggplant

Symptoms:
Yellowing between leaf veins.

Possible causes:

- Magnesium deficiency
- Root stress
- Environmental stress

Recommended actions:

- Check nutrient records
- Review irrigation schedule
- Monitor additional plants"

---

## Confidence Levels

Codex displays confidence ratings:

High confidence:
Likely issue detected.

Medium confidence:
Multiple possible causes.

Low confidence:
Insufficient information.

---

## Human Verification Rule

AI diagnosis is a decision-support tool.

It does not replace:

- Farm managers
- Agronomists
- Laboratory testing

Critical treatment decisions require human verification.

---

## Historical Analysis

Images become part of the crop history.

Management can review:

- Disease frequency
- Problem recurrence
- Treatment effectiveness
- Seasonal patterns

---

## Privacy and Cost Control

Image analysis may require external AI services.

The system records:

- AI provider used
- Processing cost
- Date and user
- Related crop records
