# AI Knowledge Assistant and SOP Training

## Purpose

Codex acts as a digital trainer and agricultural knowledge assistant.

It helps employees learn company procedures and agricultural best practices.

---

## Company Knowledge Base

The system stores internal knowledge including:

- Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
- Crop production methods
- Fertigation recipes
- Equipment instructions
- Safety procedures
- Harvest quality standards
- Packaging procedures

---

## Example Worker Questions

Worker:

"Codex, how do I prepare nutrient solution for lettuce?"

Codex provides:

- Required materials
- Mixing procedure
- Safety reminders
- Required EC range
- Common mistakes to avoid

---

## Training Assistant

Codex can help:

New employees:

- Learn farm operations
- Understand assigned tasks
- Review training materials

Experienced employees:

- Refresh procedures
- Learn new methods
- Check updated instructions

---

## Multimedia Learning

Training materials may include:

- Text instructions
- Photos
- Videos
- Diagrams
- Safety illustrations

---

## Knowledge Levels

Information may be categorized as:

Public Knowledge:

General farming information.

Company Knowledge:

Internal SOP and procedures.

Confidential Knowledge:

Proprietary systems and research.

Examples:

- Gomez Method Hydroponics
- DFG Production System
- Internal production strategies

---

## Version Control

SOP documents maintain:

- Version number
- Author
- Approval history
- Effective date

Employees always receive the latest approved procedure.

---

## AI Safety Rule

Codex should distinguish between:

Official company procedures

and

General AI agricultural advice.

Company-approved procedures always take priority.
