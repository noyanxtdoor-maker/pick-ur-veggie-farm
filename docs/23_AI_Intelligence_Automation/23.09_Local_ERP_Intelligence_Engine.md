# Local ERP Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

The Local ERP Intelligence Engine is Codex's first and most important brain.

It operates without requiring external AI services.

This ensures that PickUrVeggie ERP V3 remains intelligent even when:

- Internet is unavailable
- AI providers are offline
- AI budgets are exhausted

---

## Local Intelligence Priority Rule

Codex follows this decision order:

1. Local ERP Intelligence
2. Existing Company Knowledge Base
3. External AI Services

The system always chooses the lowest-cost and most reliable source first.

---

## Inventory Intelligence

Codex continuously monitors:

- Low stock levels
- Fast consumption rates
- Unusual usage patterns
- Expiring materials
- Missing stock movements
- FIFO violations

Examples:

"Calcinit inventory is estimated to last only 4 more days."

"Fertilizer consumption increased 25% compared with the previous month."

---

## Production Intelligence

Codex monitors:

- Crop schedules
- Plant age
- Growth stages
- Expected harvest dates
- Yield trends
- Production delays

Example:

"Eggplant Tunnel A is entering its peak fruiting period. Increase monitoring of potassium consumption."

---

## Calendar Intelligence

Codex tracks:

- Today's tasks
- Missed activities
- Overdue maintenance
- Upcoming harvests
- Employee assignments

Example:

"Two scheduled pruning activities were not completed yesterday."

---

## Financial Intelligence

Codex can identify:

- Falling profit margins
- Rising production costs
- Over-budget projects
- Late customer payments
- Unusual expenses

Example:

"Tomato Tunnel B profitability declined by 12% due to increased labor costs."

---

## HR Intelligence

Codex monitors:

- Attendance patterns
- Excessive overtime
- Leave conflicts
- Training requirements
- Contract expirations

Example:

"Three workers exceeded overtime limits this week."

---

## Rule-Based Decision Engine

The system uses predefined rules.

Example:

IF:

Inventory Remaining < 7 days

THEN:

Create Low Inventory Alert

---

## Critical Principle

Codex must provide valuable assistance even with zero external AI access.

The ERP should never become dependent on cloud AI.
