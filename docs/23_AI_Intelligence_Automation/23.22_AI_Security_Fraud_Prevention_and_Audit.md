# AI Security, Fraud Prevention and Audit

## Purpose

AI introduces powerful capabilities but also new risks.

Codex must operate under strict security rules and must never become a method for bypassing ERP controls.

---

## AI Permission Enforcement

Codex follows exactly the same permission system as the user.

Example:

A farm worker asks:

"Codex, show me all employee salaries."

Response:

"You do not have permission to access payroll information."

---

## AI Action Restrictions

Codex may never:

- Transfer money
- Approve payments
- Release salaries
- Modify accounting history
- Delete records
- Change user roles
- Disable security systems

---

## AI-Assisted Actions

Allowed workflow:

User Request

↓

Codex Analysis

↓

Draft Recommendation

↓

Human Approval

↓

ERP Execution

---

## AI Audit Trail

The ERP records:

- Who requested AI assistance
- Date and time
- AI provider used
- AI model used
- Data accessed
- Recommendation generated
- Final human decision

---

## Sensitive Data Protection

Protected information includes:

- Payroll
- Financial reports
- Customer data
- Supplier pricing
- Business strategies
- Proprietary farming methods

---

## Fraud Detection

Codex can help identify:

- Abnormal inventory losses
- Unusual employee activity
- Suspicious financial transactions
- Repeated failed approvals
- Unusual purchasing behavior

---

## AI Safety Principle

AI should increase transparency and accountability.

It must never become a shortcut that allows unauthorized actions.
