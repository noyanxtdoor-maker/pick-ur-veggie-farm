# Offline AI and Island Connectivity Strategy

## Purpose

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 is designed for island communities where internet access may be slow, unstable, or unavailable.

The ERP must remain operational even without cloud AI services.

Codex Farm Companion must gracefully switch between online and offline intelligence.

---

## Offline-First Philosophy

The farm should never stop operating because:

- Internet connection is lost
- AI providers are unavailable
- AI budget limits are reached
- Cloud services experience outages

Critical farm operations must always remain available.

---

## Offline Codex Capabilities

Local Codex intelligence can provide:

### Inventory

- Low stock alerts
- Reorder warnings
- Consumption trend calculations
- Expiry notifications

---

### Production

- Task reminders
- Crop schedules
- Harvest planning
- SOP instructions

---

### HR

- Employee schedules
- Attendance records
- Time-in and time-out logging
- Leave information

---

### Finance

- Stored reports
- Historical analytics
- Budget warnings

---

## Online AI Capabilities

External AI services are used for advanced functions:

- Pest and disease image diagnosis
- Complex business analysis
- Document understanding
- Long-term forecasting
- Advanced conversations

---

## Synchronization Strategy

When internet returns:

Offline Actions

↓


Sync Queue

↓

Validation

↓

Supabase Cloud Database

↓

Updated ERP Records

---

## Conflict Resolution

Example:

Two employees modify the same record offline.

The system must:

- Detect conflicts
- Preserve both versions
- Request supervisor review
- Maintain audit history

---

## Local Knowledge Base

The device stores:

- SOP documents
- Training guides
- Crop instructions
- Emergency procedures

This allows workers to continue learning without internet.

---

## Island Reliability Principle

A luxury farm system requires perfect internet.

A true island ERP continues operating during storms, outages, and connectivity failures.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 must be built for the realities of rural agriculture.
