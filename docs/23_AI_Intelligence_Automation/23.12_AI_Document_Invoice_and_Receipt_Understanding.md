# AI Document, Invoice and Receipt Understanding

## Purpose

Codex reduces manual data entry by reading business documents and extracting useful information.

---

## Supported Documents

Examples:

Purchasing:

- Supplier invoices
- Delivery receipts
- Quotations
- Purchase orders

Financial:

- Expense receipts
- Utility bills
- Bank documents

Operations:

- Equipment manuals
- Safety documents
- Certification records

---

## Document Processing Workflow

User uploads document.

↓

Codex scans the document.

↓

AI extracts information.

↓

Codex prepares a draft record.

↓

User reviews the data.

↓

User approves the transaction.

---

---

## Important Control Principle

AI never creates final accounting records automatically.

Examples:

Allowed:

Prepare draft purchase.

Not Allowed:

Approve supplier payment.

Post final accounting entries.

---

## OCR and AI Combination

The system may use:

- OCR for text extraction
- AI for understanding context
- ERP validation rules for accuracy checking

---

## Error Detection

Codex can warn users about:

- Duplicate invoices
- Missing information
- Incorrect quantities
- Price anomalies
- Unusual supplier charges

---

## Long-Term Benefits

The system gradually creates a searchable knowledge library containing:

- Historical purchases
- Equipment manuals
- Technical documents
- Supplier information
- Operational records
