# AI Financial and Business Intelligence

## Purpose

Codex transforms financial records into understandable business intelligence.

Traditional accounting answers:

"What happened?"

Codex answers:

"Why did it happen?"
"What will happen next?"
"What should we do about it?"

---

## Financial Data Analysis

Codex can analyze:

- Revenue trends
- Cost trends
- Gross profit margins
- Net profit margins
- Cash flow patterns
- Customer payment behavior
- Supplier expenses
- Crop profitability

## Profitability Investigation

Owner asks:

"Codex, why did eggplant profits decrease this month?"

Codex analyzes:

- Lower selling price
- Increased fertilizer usage
- Increased labor hours
- Reduced harvest yield
- Higher spoilage

Example response:

"Eggplant profit declined by 15%.
The largest contributor was a 25% increase in fertilizer cost."

---

## Cash Flow Intelligence

Codex monitors:

- Low cash balances
- Upcoming obligations
- Slow-paying customers
- Seasonal cash shortages

Example:

"Based on current projections, available cash may be insufficient next month."

---

## Customer Business Analysis

Codex identifies:

- Highest-value customers
- Customers with declining purchases
- Late-paying customers
- Customers with growth potential

Example:

"Resort A increased purchases by 40% over the last three months."

---

## Business Performance Comparison

Compare:

- Crops
- Varieties
- Farm locations
- Production methods
- Seasons
- Years

Example:

"DFG Eggplant generated 18% higher profit per square meter compared with traditional soil production."

---

## Strategic Recommendations

Codex may recommend:

- Increase production
- Reduce unprofitable crops
- Adjust pricing
- Negotiate supplier pricing
- Improve cost control

---

## Human Decision Principle

Codex provides intelligence and recommendations.

Final financial decisions always belong to management.
