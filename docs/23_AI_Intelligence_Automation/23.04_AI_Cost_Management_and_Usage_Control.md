# AI Cost Management and Usage Control

## Purpose

Artificial Intelligence is a valuable business resource that may have operating costs.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 must provide intelligent AI features while ensuring that companies maintain complete control over AI expenses.

---

## AI Cost Philosophy

AI usage should be treated similarly to:

- Electricity
- Internet
- Fuel
- Fertilizer
- Equipment usage

It must be measured, monitored, and optimized.

---

## AI Usage Levels


### Level 0 — Local ERP Intelligence (FREE)

Examples:

- Inventory shortage warnings
- Harvest reminders
- Calendar alerts
- Attendance summaries
- Financial calculations
- Basic business rules

No external AI provider is required.

---

### Level 1 — Basic AI Assistance

Examples:

- Simple conversations
- SOP explanations
- General farm questions
- Basic report explanations

May use economical AI models.

---

### Level 2 — Advanced Analysis

Examples:

- Profitability explanations
- Business analysis
- Multi-module correlations
- Operational recommendations

May use more powerful AI models.

---

### Level 3 — Premium AI Operations

Examples:

- Pest image diagnosis
- Large document analysis
- Long-term forecasting
- Advanced simulations

These may consume higher AI resources.

---

## AI Usage Tracking

Every AI request may record:

- User
- Company
- Date and time
- AI provider
- AI model
- Feature used
- Estimated cost
- Processing duration

---

## AI Budget Controls

Administrators may configure:

### Monthly AI Budget

Example:

₱2,000/month AI limit

---

### User Limits

Example:

Farm Worker:
10 AI requests/day

Supervisor:
50 AI requests/day

Owner:
Unlimited

---

## AI Transparency

Codex may inform users when advanced AI is being used.

Example:

"This analysis uses advanced AI resources."

---

## AI Availability Principle

The ERP must remain fully operational even when:

- AI budget is exhausted
- Internet is unavailable
- AI providers are unavailable

Codex automatically falls back to local ERP intelligence.
