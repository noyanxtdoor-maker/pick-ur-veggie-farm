# AI Feature Subscriptions and Monetization

## Purpose

Advanced AI features create operational costs.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 should support different AI service levels based on customer needs.

---

## Free or Basic Tier

Includes:

- Local Codex intelligence
- Inventory alerts
- Calendar reminders
- Basic analytics
- SOP assistance

External AI usage:

Optional or limited.

---

## Professional AI Tier

Includes:

- Advanced Codex conversations
- Pest and disease analysis
- Document understanding
- Business summaries
- Intelligent recommendations

---

## Enterprise AI Tier

Includes:

- Advanced forecasting
- Multi-farm intelligence
- Custom AI configuration
- Higher AI usage limits
- Priority AI processing

---

## AI Credit System

The ERP may implement:

- Monthly AI credits
- Usage quotas
- Additional AI credit purchases

Examples:

100 AI image analyses/month

or

10,000 AI conversation requests/month

---

## Company AI Controls

Owners may configure:

- AI budget limits
- Allowed AI providers
- Employee AI permissions
- Daily AI usage limits

---

## Sustainable Business Model

AI should create value that exceeds its operating cost.

The goal is not to maximize AI usage.

The goal is to maximize better decisions and farm efficiency.
