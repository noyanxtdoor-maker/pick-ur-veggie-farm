# AI Farm Monitoring and Anomaly Detection

## Purpose

Codex continuously monitors farm data to detect unusual events before they become major problems.

Traditional systems report what already happened.

Codex identifies abnormal patterns and warns the team early.

---

## Monitoring Categories

Codex analyzes:

- Crop health
- Growth progress
- Yield trends
- Inventory behavior
- Employee activities
- Equipment performance
- Financial anomalies
- Environmental conditions

---

## Crop Anomaly Detection

Examples:

Expected:

Eggplant Tunnel A normally produces:
80–100 kg/week


Actual:

This week:
45 kg


Codex Alert:

"Yield is 50% below expected performance.

Possible causes:

- Pest pressure
- Disease outbreak
- Nutrient imbalance
- Environmental stress

Recommended action:

Inspect the crop and review production records."

---

## Growth Schedule Monitoring

Codex compares actual progress against expected timelines.

Examples:

Expected:

Lettuce harvest:
30 days after transplant

Current:

Day 35:
Not ready for harvest


Alert:

"Crop maturity delay detected. Review environmental and nutrition records."

---

## Inventory Anomaly Detection

Examples:

Normal Calcinit use:

5 kg/day


Current use:

12 kg/day


Codex Alert:

"Fertilizer consumption increased 140% above normal.

Possible causes:

- Incorrect application
- Leakage
- Waste
- Theft
- Increased production activity"

---

## Employee Behavior Analysis

Codex can detect:

- Frequent absenteeism
- Excessive overtime
- Missed tasks
- Unusual attendance patterns

Example:

"Worker attendance dropped 30% this month compared with historical records."

---

## Equipment Anomaly Detection

Examples:

Water pump usually runs:

2 hours/day


Current week:

6 hours/day


Possible causes:

- Pipe leakage
- System blockage
- Mechanical failure

---

## Financial Anomaly Detection

Examples:

- Sudden cost increases
- Unusual expenses
- Profit margin reductions
- Unexpected cash shortages
- Abnormal inventory losses

---

## Alert Severity Levels

🟢 Information

Minor changes requiring awareness.


🟡 Warning

Potential problems requiring review.


🔴 Critical

Urgent issues requiring immediate action.

---

## Learning and Improvement

Codex continuously improves by analyzing historical farm patterns.

However, all recommendations remain suggestions requiring human judgment.

---

## Core Principle

The goal is to identify problems while they are still small.

Early detection saves crops, money, and labor.
