# AI HR Productivity and Workforce Analytics

## Purpose

Codex helps management understand workforce performance while maintaining employee fairness and privacy.

The objective is improvement, not surveillance.

---

## Attendance Intelligence

Codex analyzes:

- Attendance trends
- Late arrivals
- Absences
- Overtime frequency
- Time-in and time-out patterns

Example:

"Labor overtime increased 30% this month compared with the previous month."

---

## Workforce Productivity

Codex may analyze:

- Tasks completed
- Harvest output
- Labor hours per crop
- Productivity per production zone
- Equipment usage efficiency

---

## Workforce Planning

Codex assists management by predicting:

- Labor shortages
- Peak harvest workforce requirements
- Seasonal hiring needs
- Training requirements

Example:

"Expected lettuce harvest next week requires 3 additional workers."

---

## Training Intelligence

Codex identifies:

- Employees requiring additional training
- Frequently repeated mistakes
- Safety reminders
- SOP compliance issues

---

## Employee Wellness Indicators

The ERP may monitor warning signs:

- Excessive overtime
- Continuous work without rest days
- Frequent schedule conflicts

The goal is to protect employee health and maintain productivity.

---

## Permission Protection

Codex follows HR security rules.

Examples:

A worker cannot ask:

"How much salary does another employee receive?"

Codex response:

"You do not have permission to access this information."

---

## Management Benefits

The owner can understand:

- True labor cost per crop
- Workforce efficiency
- Hiring requirements
- Training opportunities
- Productivity trends
