# AI Notifications and Daily Farm Briefing

## Purpose

Codex acts as a proactive assistant.

Instead of waiting for users to search for problems, Codex informs them before issues become critical.

---

## Daily Codex Briefing

Example:

🐾 Good morning Arlie.

Farm Status:
🟢 Healthy

Today's Priorities:

🌱 Production

- Eggplant Tunnel A harvest scheduled at 7 AM.
- Lettuce Zone C requires nutrient adjustment.

📦 Inventory

- Calcium Nitrate estimated to run out in 5 days.

👨‍🌾 Workforce

- Two workers are on approved leave today.

💰 Finance

- ₱50,000 customer invoice is overdue.

🔧 Equipment

- Irrigation pump maintenance due tomorrow.

---

## Notification Categories

### Information

Routine updates and reminders.

---

### Warning

Potential problems requiring attention.

Examples:

- Low stock
- Increasing costs
- Delayed tasks

---

### Critical Alerts

Immediate action required.

Examples:

- Irrigation failure
- Major disease outbreak
- Severe financial anomalies

---

## Smart Notification Control

Users may customize:

- Notification type
- Frequency
- Delivery method
- Quiet hours

---

## Delivery Channels

Notifications may be sent through:

- In-app alerts
- Push notifications
- Email
- SMS
- Future messaging integrations

---

## Priority Management

Codex avoids unnecessary alerts.

The goal is to reduce alert fatigue and only deliver meaningful information.
