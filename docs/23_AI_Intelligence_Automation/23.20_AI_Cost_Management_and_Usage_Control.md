# AI Cost Management and Usage Control

## Purpose

Artificial Intelligence services may have usage costs.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 must ensure that AI improves business efficiency without creating uncontrolled expenses.

The system will provide AI budget monitoring, usage limits, and administrator controls.

---

# AI Cost Philosophy

AI should be treated like any other company resource.

Examples:

- Electricity
- Internet
- Fertilizer
- Fuel

AI consumption must be measurable and controllable.

---

# AI Usage Categories

The ERP classifies AI requests according to cost.

## Level 0 — Local ERP Intelligence (FREE)

Examples:

- Inventory alerts
- Harvest reminders
- Expiring stock warnings
- Cash reconciliation warnings
- Employee attendance summaries
- Basic calculations

No external AI service is required.

---

## Level 1 — Low Cost AI Requests

Examples:

- Simple farm questions
- Report explanations
- SOP assistance
- Basic conversations with Codex

The system may use low-cost AI models.

---

## Level 2 — Medium Cost AI Analysis

Examples:

- Financial trend analysis
- Production comparisons
- Crop profitability explanations
- Business summaries

Requires more advanced AI reasoning.

---

## Level 3 — High Cost AI Operations

Examples:

- Disease image diagnosis
- Large document analysis
- Multi-year farm data analysis
- Advanced forecasting and simulations

These may require premium AI models.

---

# AI Usage Tracking

Every AI interaction may record:

- User account
- Company
- Date and time
- AI feature used
- AI provider used
- Model used
- Estimated token usage
- Estimated cost
- Response duration

# AI Approval Workflow

Expensive AI requests may require approval.

Example:

Worker requests a large crop analysis.

The ERP displays:

"This request may consume premium AI credits."

Options:

- Request approval
- Continue using available budget
- Cancel request

# AI Provider Management

The ERP may support multiple AI providers.

Examples:

- OpenAI
- Google Gemini
- Future AI services

Administrators can configure:

- Preferred provider
- Backup provider
- Allowed AI features
- Cost limits

---

# Offline AI Strategy

The ERP must remain functional even if:

- Internet connection is lost
- AI provider services are unavailable
- AI budget has been exhausted

Codex will automatically fall back to:

- Local rules
- Existing ERP data
- Stored knowledge
- Standard operating procedures

---

# AI Transparency

Codex should be able to explain when an external AI service was used.

Example:

"This diagnosis was generated using advanced AI image analysis."

This helps users understand the source of recommendations.

---

# AI Security Rules

AI must never be allowed to:

- Transfer money
- Approve payments
- Modify accounting records
- Change payroll
- Delete inventory history
- Override user permissions

Critical business actions always require authorized human approval.

---

# AI Business Intelligence Principle

The goal of AI is not to replace farmers, workers, or managers.

The goal is to give every person a smarter assistant that helps them make better decisions.

---

# Final Architecture Principle

Codex Farm Companion is the face of the AI system.

External AI providers are invisible engines behind Codex.

The ERP must always be useful even with zero external AI availability.
