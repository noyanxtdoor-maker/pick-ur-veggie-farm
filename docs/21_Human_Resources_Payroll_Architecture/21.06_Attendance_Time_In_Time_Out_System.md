# Attendance Time In and Time Out System

Attendance is the foundation of payroll computation.

All attendance records automatically connect to the payroll engine.

---

## Time In Workflow

Employee opens the application.

Select:

[ TIME IN ]

The system records:

- Employee ID
- Date
- Time
- GPS location (optional)
- Device information
- Photo verification (optional)

Status:

Present

---

## Time Out Workflow

Employee selects:

[ TIME OUT ]

The system records:

- Time out
- Total hours worked
- Break deductions
- Overtime hours

---

## Attendance Methods

The company may enable:

1. Mobile application check-in
2. Tablet kiosk mode
3. QR code scanning
4. Supervisor attendance entry
5. Biometric integration (future)

---

## Attendance Exceptions

The system handles:

- Late arrival
- Early departure
- Missing time out
- Duplicate attendance
- Unauthorized attendance changes

---

## Correction Requests

Employees cannot directly edit attendance records.

They may submit a correction request:

Example:

"Forgot to time out yesterday."

The request goes to a supervisor or administrator for approval.

---

## Payroll Integration

Attendance affects:

- Daily wage computation
- Hourly salary
- Overtime calculations
- Absence deductions
- Late penalties
- Holiday payments

All changes are recorded in the audit log.
