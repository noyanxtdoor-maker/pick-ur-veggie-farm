# Employee Performance and Evaluations

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 includes a structured performance management system to help identify high-performing employees, training needs, promotion opportunities, and operational improvements.

The goal is not only to evaluate employees but also to develop their skills and improve farm productivity.

---

## Performance Evaluation Categories

Employees may be evaluated based on:

### Productivity

Examples:

- Harvest quantity completed
- Assigned tasks completed
- Accuracy of work
- Speed and efficiency

---

### Quality of Work

Examples:

- Harvest quality
- Proper handling of crops
- Correct inventory procedures
- Compliance with farm standards

---

### Attendance & Reliability

Measured through:

- Attendance records
- Punctuality
- Absences
- Consistency

---

### Teamwork & Attitude

Examples:

- Cooperation with other workers
- Respect toward supervisors
- Communication
- Initiative

---

### Safety & Compliance

Examples:

- Following farm safety procedures
- Proper use of equipment
- Following chemical handling rules
- Maintaining cleanliness standards

---

## Performance Scoring

The company may use a scoring system:

1 - Poor

2 - Needs Improvement

3 - Meets Expectations

4 - Exceeds Expectations

5 - Outstanding

---

## Evaluation Frequency

Configurable periods:

- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Semi-annual
- Annual
- Special evaluations

---

## Rewards and Development

Performance records may support:

- Bonuses
- Salary increases
- Promotions
- Additional responsibilities
- Training recommendations

---

## Audit History

Performance records are historical records.

Old evaluations cannot be deleted or modified without authorized approval and audit logging.
