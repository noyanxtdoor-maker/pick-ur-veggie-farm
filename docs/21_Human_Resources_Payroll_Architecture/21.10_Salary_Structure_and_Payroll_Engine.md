# Salary Structure and Payroll Engine

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 supports flexible compensation structures suitable for agricultural workers, office employees, operators, managers, and executives.

The payroll engine must accommodate different salary arrangements while maintaining accurate financial records.

---

## Salary Types

The system supports:

### 1. Daily Wage

Example:

Juan Santos

Daily Rate:
₱500/day

Payroll calculation:

Days Worked × Daily Rate

---

### 2. Monthly Salary

Example:

Farm Administrator

Monthly Salary:
₱25,000/month

Payroll is calculated based on:

- Attendance
- Leave policies
- Absences
- Overtime
- Deductions

---

### 3. Hourly Rate

Example:

Temporary laborer

Hourly Rate:
₱80/hour

Payroll calculation:

Hours Worked × Hourly Rate

---

### 4. Piece Rate

Used for agricultural activities where payment depends on production output.

Examples:

- Harvesting per kilogram
- Packaging per box
- Seedling preparation per tray

Example:

Harvest Rate:
₱3 per kilogram

Total Harvest:
500 kg

Salary:
500 × ₱3 = ₱1,500

---

## Compensation Components

Salary may include:

### Earnings

- Basic salary
- Overtime pay
- Holiday pay
- Night differential
- Production incentives
- Performance bonuses
- Allowances

---

### Deductions

- Cash advances
- Salary advances
- Absences
- Late penalties
- Government contributions
- Taxes
- Other company deductions

---

## Salary History

A salary record must never be overwritten.

Example:

2026:
Daily Rate ₱500

2027:
Daily Rate ₱600

The system keeps historical salary records to ensure old payroll calculations remain accurate.

---

## Payroll Philosophy

Payroll is an accounting representation of labor cost.

The ERP records:

- How much is owed
- How much has been paid
- Outstanding balances

The ERP does NOT store or transfer actual money.
