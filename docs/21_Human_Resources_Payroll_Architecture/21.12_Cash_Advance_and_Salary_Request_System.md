# Cash Advance and Salary Request System

Employees may request financial assistance through the ERP.

The system supports both:

- Employee-initiated requests
- Management-created entries

---

## Cash Advance Request

Employee Workflow:

Employee submits request:

Amount:
₱3,000

Reason:
Family emergency

Status:

Pending Approval

↓

Management Review

↓

Approved / Rejected

---

## Management-Initiated Cash Advance

Authorized roles:

- Developer
- Owner
- Co-owner

may create a cash advance record directly for an employee.

Example:

Employee:
Juan Santos

Cash Advance:
₱5,000

Approved by:
Owner

---

## Salary Advance Request

Employees may request a portion of their earned salary before payroll date.

Example:

Earned Salary:
₱10,000

Requested Advance:
₱4,000

---

## Validation Rules

The system may enforce:

- Maximum advance limits
- Minimum working period
- Outstanding advance checks
- Approval hierarchy

---

## Payroll Integration

Approved advances become deductions during payroll.

Example:

Net Salary:
₱8,000

Cash Advance:
₱2,000

Amount to Pay:
₱6,000

---

## Financial Principle

The ERP only records the transaction.

Actual cash movement occurs outside the system through:

- Cash
- GCash
- Maya
- Bank transfer
- Check
