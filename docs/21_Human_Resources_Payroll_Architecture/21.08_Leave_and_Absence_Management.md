# Leave and Absence Management

Employees can request official absences through the ERP.

---

## Leave Types

Examples:

- Vacation Leave
- Sick Leave
- Emergency Leave
- Maternity Leave
- Paternity Leave
- Unpaid Leave
- Other company-specific leave

---

## Leave Request Workflow

Employee submits request.

Provides:

- Leave type
- Start date
- End date
- Reason
- Supporting documents (optional)

Status:

Pending Approval

↓

Supervisor Review

↓

Approved or Rejected

---

## Calendar Integration

Approved leave automatically blocks work schedules.

Example:

Juan Santos
Vacation Leave

June 10 - June 15

The scheduling system prevents assigning Juan to farm tasks during this period.

---

## Absence Tracking

The system categorizes:

- Approved leave
- Unexcused absence
- Sick absence
- Late attendance
- Early departure

---

## Payroll Connection

Leave records determine:

- Paid leave compensation
- Unpaid deductions
- Attendance calculations
