# Disciplinary Action and Incident Records

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 maintains a structured disciplinary and incident management system.

The purpose is to protect both the company and employees by ensuring that all incidents are documented, investigated, and resolved fairly.

---

## Incident Types

Examples:

### Attendance Violations

- Repeated lateness
- Unauthorized absences
- Falsified attendance records

---

### Operational Violations

- Failure to follow farm procedures
- Incorrect fertilizer preparation
- Improper harvesting methods
- Negligent handling of equipment
- Failure to follow safety protocols

---

### Inventory and Financial Violations

Examples:

- Unauthorized inventory withdrawals
- Missing materials
- Fraud attempts
- Unauthorized cash handling
- False expense claims

---

### Workplace Conduct

Examples:

- Harassment
- Violence
- Threats
- Disrespectful behavior
- Substance abuse during work

---

## Incident Reporting Workflow

Employee or Supervisor reports incident

↓

Incident Record Created

↓

Evidence Attached

↓

Management Investigation

↓

Decision Recorded

↓

Action Implemented

---

## Evidence Management

Records may include:

- Written statements
- Photos
- Videos
- Witness reports
- Supporting documents

---

## Disciplinary Actions

Possible actions:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Suspension
- Mandatory retraining
- Financial liability (where legally applicable)
- Termination

---

## Fairness and Approval

No disciplinary record may be finalized without:

- Investigation
- Responsible manager approval
- Reason documentation
- Audit logging

---

## Record Retention

Disciplinary history remains stored for legal, operational, and audit purposes.

Records cannot be secretly edited or deleted.
