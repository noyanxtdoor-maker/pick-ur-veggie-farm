# Payroll Approval Workflow

Payroll requires multiple levels of authorization to prevent errors and fraud.

---

## Payroll Processing Workflow

HR/Admin prepares payroll
            ↓
System validates records
            ↓
Management reviews payroll
            ↓
Approve or reject payroll
            ↓
Payroll is locked
            ↓
Payment can be recorded

---

## Approval Authority

Approval rights may include:

- Owner
- Co-owner
- Authorized finance administrators

Permissions are configurable by company policy.

---

## Validation Checks

Before approval, the system checks:

- Missing attendance records
- Duplicate employees
- Negative salary amounts
- Excessive overtime
- Unauthorized deductions
- Duplicate payroll periods

---

## Rejected Payroll

If rejected:

The payroll returns to draft status.

Corrections may be made.

The system records:

- Who rejected it
- Date and time
- Reason for rejection

---

## Audit Trail

Every payroll action is permanently recorded:

- Creation
- Modification
- Approval
- Rejection
- Payment status changes
