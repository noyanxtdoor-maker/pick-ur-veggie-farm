# Employee Account Creation Workflow

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 supports two account creation methods.

---

## Method A: Management Creates Employee Account

Workflow:

HR / Owner creates employee record
↓
System generates employee ID
↓
Username and temporary password created
↓
Credentials given to employee
↓
Employee logs in
↓
Employee changes password

---

## Method B: Employee Self Registration

Workflow:

Employee signs up
↓
Account remains pending
↓
Management reviews identity
↓
Account linked to employee record
↓
Role permissions assigned
↓
Account activated

---

## Recommended Model

The ERP supports both methods.

This provides flexibility for:

- Farm workers
- Office staff
- Managers
- Seasonal employees

---

## Security Rules

No user receives access until:

- Identity verification is completed.
- Role permissions are assigned.
- Company approval is granted.
