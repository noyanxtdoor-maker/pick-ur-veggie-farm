# Employee Workspace and Profile

Every employee receives a private personal workspace inside PickUrVeggie ERP V3.

The workspace allows employees to view and manage their own employment information without exposing other employees' data.

---

## Employee Dashboard

The employee dashboard includes:

- Today's assigned tasks
- Work schedule
- Attendance status
- Upcoming shifts
- Notifications
- Personal reminders

Example:

Good Morning, Juan Santos

TODAY'S WORK

06:00 AM - Check DFG Irrigation Tunnel A
08:00 AM - Harvest Eggplant Block B
02:00 PM - Packaging and Sorting

Attendance Status:
✓ Time In Completed

---

## My Profile

Employees can view:

- Employee ID
- Name
- Position
- Department
- Employment status
- Contact information
- Emergency contacts

---

## Editable Information

Employees may request updates for:

- Phone number
- Address
- Emergency contact
- Profile photo

Certain critical information cannot be changed by the employee:

- Salary
- Position
- Role permissions
- Employment status

These require management approval.

---

## Personal Records

Employees can privately access:

- Attendance history
- Leave history
- Payroll records
- Payslips
- Cash advance history
- Salary requests
- Submitted documents

---

## Privacy Principle

Employees cannot view:

- Other employee records
- Other employee salaries
- Management notes
- Company confidential information

Every user only has access to their own workspace.
