# Overtime and Holiday Pay System

Agricultural operations often require extended working hours during harvest periods.

The ERP calculates overtime and special compensation based on company rules.

---

## Overtime Detection

The system compares:

Actual working hours

versus

Assigned shift hours

Example:

Scheduled shift:
06:00 AM - 03:00 PM

Actual time out:
05:00 PM

Overtime:
2 hours

---

## Overtime Approval

Overtime may require approval.

Workflow:

Employee completes extra work

↓

System records overtime

↓

Supervisor approves or rejects

↓

Approved overtime enters payroll

---

## Holiday Work

The ERP supports:

- Regular holidays
- Special holidays
- Rest days
- Company holidays

---

## Compensation Rules

The company may configure:

- Overtime multipliers
- Holiday rates
- Night differentials
- Special allowances

The system must allow different labor laws based on country or company policy.

---

## Audit Protection

Overtime records cannot be modified after payroll processing without authorized approval.

All adjustments are stored in the audit trail.
