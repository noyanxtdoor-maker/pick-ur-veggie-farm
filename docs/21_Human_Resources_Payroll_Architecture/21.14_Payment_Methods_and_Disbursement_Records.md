# Payment Methods and Disbursement Records

The ERP records how employee salaries are released.

The ERP is not a bank or payment processor.

It only maintains financial records.

---

## Supported Payment Methods

The company may record:

- Cash
- GCash
- Maya
- Bank transfer
- Check
- Other custom methods

---

## Payment Record Details

Each payment record contains:

- Employee name
- Payroll period
- Amount paid
- Payment method
- Reference number
- Date and time
- Person who recorded payment
- Notes

## Partial Payments

The ERP supports situations where salaries are paid in parts.

Example:

Total Salary:
₱15,000

First Payment:
₱5,000

Remaining Balance:
₱10,000

Status:
Partially Paid

---

## Payroll Status

Possible statuses:

- Draft
- Pending Approval
- Approved
- Unpaid
- Partially Paid
- Fully Paid

---

## Reconciliation

Financial records should periodically be compared against:

- Actual cash on hand
- Bank statements
- Digital wallet transaction history

Differences must be investigated and documented.
