# Employee Master Records

Every employee receives a permanent Employee ID.

Example:

EMP-2026-0001

The Employee Master Record contains:

## Personal Information

- Full name
- Employee ID
- Photo
- Birth date
- Address
- Contact details
- Emergency contact

---

## Employment Information

- Hiring date
- Department
- Position
- Employment type:

  - Regular
  - Probationary
  - Contract
  - Seasonal
  - Part-time

- Supervisor
- Assigned farm location

---

## Compensation Information

- Salary type:
  - Daily wage
  - Monthly salary
  - Hourly rate
  - Piece rate

- Basic salary
- Allowances
- Standard working hours

---

## Status Tracking

Employee status:

- Active
- On leave
- Suspended
- Resigned
- Terminated

---

## Historical Integrity

Employee records are never deleted.

Former employees remain in the system for:

- Payroll history
- Accounting audits
- Government compliance
- Operational records
