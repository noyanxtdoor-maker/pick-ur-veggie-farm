# HR Stress Test Scenarios

Before implementation, the HR system must be tested against difficult real-world situations.

The objective is to identify weaknesses, fraud risks, and workflow failures.

---

## Scenario 1:
Employee Forgets to Time Out

Problem:

A worker completes the day but forgets to record Time Out.

Expected System Behavior:

- Mark attendance as incomplete
- Notify employee and supervisor
- Require correction request
- Require approval before payroll adjustment

---

## Scenario 2:
Employee Requests a Cash Advance

Problem:

Employee requests ₱5,000 but already has unpaid advances.

Expected System Behavior:

- Display outstanding balance
- Apply company approval rules
- Allow management decision
- Record all approvals and rejections

---

## Scenario 3:
Supervisor Attempts Unauthorized Payroll Changes

Expected System Behavior:

- Block unauthorized changes
- Record attempt in audit logs
- Notify authorized administrators if required

---

## Scenario 4:
Employee Resigns With Company Property

Expected System Behavior:

- Prevent final clearance
- Show unreturned assets
- Require management resolution

---

## Scenario 5:
Payroll Already Approved But Error Found

Expected System Behavior:

- Prevent direct editing
- Require payroll adjustment workflow
- Maintain original payroll history
- Create audit records

---

## Scenario 6:
Seasonal Worker Returns Next Season

Expected System Behavior:

Do not create a duplicate employee.

The ERP should:

- Reactivate the existing employee record
- Preserve previous history
- Create a new employment period

---

## Scenario 7:
No Internet During Field Attendance

Expected System Behavior:

- Allow offline attendance recording
- Store records locally
- Automatically synchronize once internet returns

---

## Final HR Architecture Principle

Employees are not temporary data.

Every worker interaction with the company creates historical information that affects:

- Operations
- Payroll
- Accounting
- Security
- Compliance
- Business analytics

Therefore, employee history must be protected, traceable, and auditable throughout the lifetime of the ERP.
