# Employee Exit and Termination Workflow

Employee separation must be handled through a controlled workflow to protect company assets and maintain historical records.

Employees may leave due to:

- Resignation
- End of contract
- Retirement
- Termination
- Seasonal contract completion

---

## Exit Workflow

Separation request initiated

↓

Management review

↓

Final attendance verification

↓

Payroll and financial settlement

↓

Return of company assets

↓

Account access disabled

↓

Employee status changed to inactive

---

## Final Payroll Processing

Before an employee leaves, the system checks:

- Outstanding salary
- Overtime pay
- Leave balances
- Cash advances
- Salary advances
- Company liabilities

---

## Asset Clearance Checklist

Examples:

- Company uniforms
- Tablets
- Mobile devices
- Tools
- Equipment
- Vehicle keys
- Access cards

---

## Account Handling

The employee account is never permanently deleted.

Instead:

Status:
Inactive

Reason:
Resigned / Terminated / Contract Ended

---

## Historical Integrity

Past records remain connected to the employee:

- Attendance history
- Payroll records
- Inventory transactions
- Production activities
- Incident reports
- Performance evaluations

This protects audit accuracy and company history.
