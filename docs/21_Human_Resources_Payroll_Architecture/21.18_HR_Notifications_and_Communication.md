# HR Notifications and Communication

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 provides internal HR communication and automated notifications.

The objective is to reduce missed deadlines, improve employee awareness, and automate administrative reminders.

---

## Employee Notifications

Examples:

- New work schedule assigned
- Shift changes
- Leave approval or rejection
- Payroll availability
- Cash advance decision
- Training schedule
- Document expiration reminders

---

## Management Notifications

Examples:

- Employee absent without notice
- Overtime approval required
- Leave request waiting approval
- Contract expiration approaching
- Certification expiration
- Payroll awaiting approval

---

## Notification Channels

The system may deliver notifications through:

- In-app notifications
- Dashboard alerts
- Email
- SMS (future integration)
- Messaging integrations (future)

---

## Internal Communication

The ERP may include internal communication features such as:

- Team announcements
- Department notices
- Emergency alerts
- Company updates

---

## Privacy Rules

Messages and notifications must follow role-based permissions.

Examples:

A farm worker cannot access confidential management announcements.

Executives can send company-wide announcements.

Private employee matters remain visible only to authorized users.
