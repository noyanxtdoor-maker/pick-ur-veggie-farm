# Work Shifts and Scheduling

PickUrVeggie ERP supports flexible agricultural work schedules.

---

## Shift Types

Examples:

Regular Day Shift
06:00 AM - 03:00 PM

Night Shift
10:00 PM - 06:00 AM

Half-Day Shift
06:00 AM - 10:00 AM

Seasonal Harvest Shift
Custom schedule based on farm needs.

---

## Shift Assignment

Management can assign shifts based on:

- Employee
- Team
- Department
- Farm location
- Crop activity

Example:

Harvest Team Alpha

Monday - Friday
06:00 AM - 03:00 PM

Assigned Area:
Greenhouse Tunnel A

---

## Calendar Integration

Approved shifts automatically appear in:

Company Calendar

Visible to:
- Assigned employees
- Supervisors
- Operators

---

## Shift Conflict Detection

The system warns if:

- Employee has overlapping shifts
- Employee is assigned during approved leave
- Working hours exceed company limits

---

## Seasonal Flexibility

Agriculture requires flexible labor.

The ERP supports:

- Temporary schedules
- Harvest emergency shifts
- Weather-related schedule changes
- Additional labor assignments
