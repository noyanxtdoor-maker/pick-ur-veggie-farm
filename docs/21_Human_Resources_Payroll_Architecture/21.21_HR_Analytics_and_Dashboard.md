# HR Analytics and Dashboard

The HR dashboard provides management with real-time workforce intelligence.

The goal is to help management make better labor, productivity, and payroll decisions.

---

## Workforce Summary

Display:

- Total employees
- Active employees
- Employees on leave
- Seasonal workers
- New hires
- Upcoming contract expirations

---

## Attendance Analytics

Track:

- Daily attendance percentage
- Absence rates
- Frequent late arrivals
- Overtime hours
- Employee punctuality trends

---

## Payroll Analytics

Display:

- Total payroll cost
- Payroll by department
- Overtime expense
- Cash advances outstanding
- Salary trends over time

---

## Productivity Analytics

Connect with production modules.

Examples:

Harvest Team A:

Harvest output:
5,000 kg

₱50,000

---

## Training Analytics

Track:

- Training completion rates
- Certification expiration risks
- Employee skill distribution
