# Payroll Computation Workflow

Payroll computation transforms attendance and compensation rules into employee earnings.

---

## Payroll Flow

Attendance Records
        ↓
Shift Analysis
        ↓
Regular Hours Calculation
        ↓
Overtime Calculation
        ↓
Allowances & Incentives
        ↓
Deductions
        ↓
Gross Salary
        ↓
Net Pay
        ↓
Payroll Approval
        ↓
Payment Recording

---

## Payroll Periods

The company can configure:

- Weekly payroll
- Bi-weekly payroll
- Semi-monthly payroll
- Monthly payroll

---

## Example Payroll Computation

Employee:
Juan Santos

Period:
June 1–15, 2026

Regular Pay:
₱7,500

Overtime:
₱1,000

Allowance:
₱500

Gross Pay:
₱9,000

Deductions:

Cash Advance:
₱1,500

Late Penalty:
₱200

Net Pay:

₱9,000 - ₱1,700 = ₱7,300

---

## Payroll Locking

Once payroll is approved:

- Attendance records are locked
- Salary rates are locked
- Deductions are locked

Changes require:

- Authorized approval
- Reason for adjustment
- Audit trail

---

## Accounting Integration

Approved payroll creates accounting records:

Example:

Labor Expense       + ₱50,000

Salary Payable      + ₱50,000

When payment is made:

Salary Payable      - ₱50,000

Cash/Bank/Wallet    - ₱50,000
