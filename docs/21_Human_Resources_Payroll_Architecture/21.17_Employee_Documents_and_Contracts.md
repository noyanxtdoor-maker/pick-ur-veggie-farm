# Employee Documents and Contracts

The ERP maintains a secure digital employee file.

---

## Employee Documents

Examples:

- Employment contract
- Government identification
- Tax documents
- Medical clearance
- Training certificates
- Performance evaluations
- Disciplinary notices
- Resignation letters

---

## Document Storage

Documents may include:

- PDF files
- Images
- Scanned paperwork
- Digital agreements

The system records:

- Document type
- Upload date
- Uploaded by
- Expiration date (if applicable)
- Notes

---

## Access Control

Employee documents contain sensitive information.

Access must be restricted based on permissions.

Examples:

Employee:
Can view own allowed documents.

Supervisor:
Can view operationally necessary records.

Owner/Authorized HR:
Can access complete personnel files.

---

## Contract Management

Employment contracts track:

- Start date
- End date
- Position
- Salary agreement
- Working schedule
- Special terms

---

## Contract Expiration Alerts

The ERP sends notifications when:

- Contract expiration is approaching.
- Required documents are missing.
- Licenses or permits are about to expire.
