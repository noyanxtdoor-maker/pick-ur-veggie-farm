# Training, Certification, and Skills Management

Agricultural operations require workers with different skill levels.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 maintains a complete employee competency record.

---

## Skills Registry

Examples of employee skills:

- Hydroponic operation
- DFG system management
- Fertigation preparation
- EC and pH testing
- Pest identification
- Safe chemical application
- Harvest grading
- Inventory handling
- Equipment operation
- Vehicle operation

---

## Training Records

The system records:

- Training title
- Training provider
- Training date
- Trainer
- Training materials
- Completion status
- Evaluation results

---

## Certifications

Examples:

- Pesticide handling certificate
- Food safety certificate
- Equipment operation license
- First aid certification
- Driver's license

Each certificate records:

- Certificate number
- Issue date
- Expiration date
- Issuing organization
- Attached documents

---

## Expiration Alerts

The ERP automatically generates reminders before certifications expire.

Example:

"Juan Santos' pesticide handling certificate expires in 30 days."

---

## Workforce Planning

Management can search employees by skills.

Example:

Need an employee certified for pesticide spraying.

System displays:

Available certified operators.

---

## Business Advantage

Skill tracking allows management to:

- Assign the right people to the right jobs.
- Reduce operational mistakes.
- Improve worker development.
- Maintain regulatory compliance.
