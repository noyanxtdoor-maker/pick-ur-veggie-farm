# HR System Philosophy

The PickUrVeggie ERP V3 Human Resource system is designed around the principle that employees are valuable organizational assets.

The system tracks the complete employee lifecycle:

Recruitment
↓
Hiring
↓
Onboarding
↓
Daily Attendance
↓
Task Performance
↓
Payroll
↓
Training
↓
Promotion or Role Changes
↓
Separation / Termination

---

## Design Goals

- Minimize manual paperwork.
- Provide accurate attendance records.
- Automate payroll calculations.
- Protect employee privacy.
- Maintain complete audit history.

---

## Human-Centered Design

Field workers often use tablets under sunlight with dirty or wet hands.

Therefore:

- Large buttons.
- Minimal typing.
- One-tap attendance.
- QR code attendance support.
- Offline capability.

---

## Security Principle

Not every manager can access all employee information.

Access is controlled by:

- Role
- Department
- Assigned permissions
- Approval level
