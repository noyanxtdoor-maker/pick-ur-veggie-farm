# Accounts Receivable Customer Ledger

## Purpose

Accounts Receivable (AR) tracks money owed by customers.

This is primarily used for:

- Wholesale customers
- Resorts
- Restaurants
- Hotels
- Business clients

Retail cash sales normally create immediate payment and no receivable.

---

## Customer Ledger Structure

Each customer has a complete financial history.

Example:

Customer:
Siargao Resort Group

Opening Balance:
₱0


Invoice:
INV-2026-001
Vegetable Supply

Amount:
₱15,000


Payment:
OR-2026-001

Amount Received:
₱10,000


Remaining Balance:
₱5,000

---

## Credit Control Features

Each customer may have:

- Credit limit
- Payment terms
- Due dates
- Discount agreements
- Special pricing agreements

---

## Aging Analysis

The ERP categorizes unpaid invoices:

Current:
0–30 days

Late:
31–60 days

Overdue:
61–90 days

Critical:
90+ days

---

## Collection Management

The system may generate:

- Payment reminders
- Overdue alerts
- Customer statements
- Collection reports

---

## Payment Application

Payments must be linked to specific invoices.

Example:

Customer pays ₱20,000.

The system determines:

- Which invoices are being paid
- Partial payments
- Remaining balances

---

## Bad Debt Controls

If a customer cannot pay:

The system does not delete the invoice.

Management must process:

- Write-offs
- Payment restructuring
- Legal collection status

All actions require authorization and audit logging.
