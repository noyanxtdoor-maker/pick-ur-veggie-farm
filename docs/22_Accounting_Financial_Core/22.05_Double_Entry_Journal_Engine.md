# Double Entry Journal Engine

## Purpose

The Journal Engine converts ERP activities into balanced accounting entries.

Every transaction has two sides:

Debit

and

Credit

The total amount of debits must always equal the total amount of credits.

---

## Common Journal Transactions

### Purchase of Inventory

Example:

Buy seeds worth ₱10,000 on credit.

Debit:
1210 Seed Inventory              ₱10,000

Credit:
2110 Supplier Payable            ₱10,000

---

### Material Consumption

Use ₱500 fertilizer for Eggplant Tunnel A.

Debit:
1320 Crop Work-in-Progress       ₱500

Credit:
1220 Fertilizer Inventory        ₱500

---

### Harvest Transfer

Transfer crop value to finished inventory.

Debit:
1350 Harvest Finished Goods      ₱5,000

Credit:
1330 Productive Biological Asset ₱5,000

---

### Sale of Produce

Customer buys vegetables worth ₱8,000.

Revenue Entry:

Debit:
1100 Cash / 1140 Accounts Receivable  ₱8,000

Credit:
4100 Sales Revenue                    ₱8,000


Cost Release Entry:

Debit:
5100 Cost of Goods Sold               ₱3,000

Credit:
1350 Harvest Inventory                ₱3,000

---

## Journal Numbering

Example format:

JE-2026-000001

Where:

JE = Journal Entry

2026 = Fiscal Year

000001 = Sequential Number

---

## Manual Journal Entries

Manual journals are allowed only for authorized financial users.

Examples:

- Opening balances
- Correcting entries
- Asset adjustments
- Accountant adjustments

---

## Manual Journal Controls

The system requires:

- Reason for entry
- Supporting documents
- Approval workflow
- Complete audit history

---

## Forbidden Actions

The ERP will not allow:

- Unbalanced journals
- Deleted approved journals
- Backdated entries in locked periods
- Unauthorized adjustments
