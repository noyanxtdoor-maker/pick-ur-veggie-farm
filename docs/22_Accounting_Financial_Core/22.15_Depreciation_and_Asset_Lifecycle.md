# Depreciation and Asset Lifecycle

## Purpose

Long-term assets lose value over time due to:

- Usage
- Wear and tear
- Aging
- Obsolescence

The ERP records this reduction through depreciation.

---

## Example

Greenhouse Cost:

₱1,200,000

Useful Life:

10 years

Estimated Residual Value:

₱200,000

Depreciable Amount:

₱1,000,000

Annual Depreciation:

₱100,000 per year

---

## Depreciation Methods

The ERP may support:

- Straight-line depreciation
- Declining balance
- Units of production

The selected method depends on accounting policy.

---

## Asset Improvement vs Repair

Important distinction:

Repair:

Maintains existing condition.

Example:

Replace a damaged hose.

Usually recorded as expense.

---

Improvement:

Extends life or increases capacity.

Example:

Upgrade a greenhouse with automated climate control.

May be added to asset value.

---

## Asset Monitoring

The ERP tracks:

- Original cost
- Accumulated depreciation
- Current book value
- Maintenance history
- Repair costs
- Remaining useful life

---

## Replacement Intelligence

The system can warn management when:

- Repair costs become excessive
- An asset reaches the end of useful life
- Downtime becomes frequent

This supports better investment decisions.
