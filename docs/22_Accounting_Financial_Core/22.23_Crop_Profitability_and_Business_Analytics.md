# Crop Profitability and Business Analytics

## Purpose

Traditional accounting tells the owner whether the company made money.

Agricultural intelligence tells the owner WHY it made or lost money.

---

## Profitability Formula


Revenue

minus

Direct Production Costs

minus

Allocated Overhead

equals

True Crop Profit


---

## Profitability Levels

The ERP can analyze profitability by:

- Individual crop
- Variety
- Production block
- Greenhouse
- Tunnel
- Farm location
- Season
- Customer type

## Comparative Analytics

Management can compare:

Example:

Crop            ROI

Eggplant        50%

Lettuce         35%

Tomato          25%


The system helps decide:

- Which crops to expand
- Which crops to discontinue
- Which varieties perform better
- Which growing methods are most efficient

## Strategic Goal

The owner should not simply ask:

"How much did we harvest?"

The owner should be able to ask:

"Which crop, in which location, using which method, gave me the highest return on investment?"

The ERP should provide the answer.
