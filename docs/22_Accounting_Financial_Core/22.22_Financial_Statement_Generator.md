# Financial Statement Generator

## Purpose

The ultimate purpose of accounting is to convert millions of transactions into meaningful financial information.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 automatically generates financial reports from validated accounting records.

---

## Main Financial Reports

The system generates:

- Trial Balance
- General Ledger Report
- Profit and Loss Statement
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement

## Reporting Filters

Management may view reports by:

- Date range
- Farm location
- Crop type
- Production block
- Department
- Business unit

---

## Financial Integrity Rules

Reports only use:

- Approved transactions
- Posted journal entries
- Open accounting periods

Draft transactions are excluded.
