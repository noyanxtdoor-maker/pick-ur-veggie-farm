# Accounting Philosophy

## Accounting Is the Digital Financial Memory of the Business

Operations tell us what happened.

Accounting tells us what it cost and whether the business earned money.

---

↓
Inventory Asset

↓

Growing Biological Asset

Profit or Loss

---

## Avoiding Incorrect Farm Accounting

A common mistake:

Buying ₱50,000 fertilizer today and immediately recording it as an expense.

This creates false losses.

Correct treatment:

Purchase:

Increase fertilizer inventory.

Use:

Transfer cost into crop production.

Sale:

Recognize cost when the crop is sold.

---

## Matching Principle

Costs should be recognized in the same period as the revenue they helped create.

Example:

A tomato crop may require four months of inputs before any sale occurs.

The ERP accumulates those costs and releases them properly over the harvest lifecycle.

---

## Agricultural Accounting Principles

The system supports:

- Inventory accounting principles
- Biological asset accounting concepts under IAS 41
- Historical cost tracking
- FIFO inventory release
- Accurate crop profitability analysis

## Ultimate Objective

The owner should be able to answer:

- Which crop makes the most money?
- Which tunnel has the highest profit?
- Why did profit decline this month?
- Which inputs are becoming too expensive?
- Are we growing the right crops?

The accounting engine provides these answers.
