# Harvest FIFO COGS Release Engine

## Purpose

Harvested crops are treated as finished goods inventory.

Their cost remains an asset until the product is sold or discarded.

---

## Harvest Batch Creation

Every harvest creates a unique batch.

Example:

HB-20260620-T1-EGG-001

The batch records:

- Harvest date
- Crop block
- Quantity
- Grade
- Unit cost
- Storage location
- Expiration date


---

## FIFO Sales Allocation

The system follows:

First In, First Out (FIFO)


Example:


Available Inventory:


Batch A

Harvest:
June 1

Quantity:
100 kg

Cost:
₱20/kg


Batch B

Harvest:
June 5

Quantity:
150 kg

Cost:
₱25/kg


Customer order:

120 kg


Allocation:

100 kg from Batch A

20 kg from Batch B


---

## Cost of Goods Sold Calculation


Batch A:

100 × ₱20 = ₱2,000


Batch B:

20 × ₱25 = ₱500


Total COGS:

₱2,500


Accounting Entry:


Debit:

Cost of Goods Sold     ₱2,500


Credit:

Harvest Inventory      ₱2,500


---

## Unsold or Expired Produce


Examples:

- Spoilage
- Rot
- Quality failure


Accounting Entry:


Debit:

Inventory Spoilage Expense


Credit:

Harvest Inventory


---

## Traceability Integration

Every sale can be traced backward:

Customer Invoice

        ↓

Harvest Batch

        ↓

Crop Block

        ↓

Production Records

        ↓

Seed Lot

        ↓

Supplier


---

## ERP Integrity Rule

No harvested inventory may be sold without:

- Available stock
- Valid batch
- Proper FIFO allocation
- Cost calculation
- Audit record


This prevents inventory fraud and inaccurate profitability reports.
