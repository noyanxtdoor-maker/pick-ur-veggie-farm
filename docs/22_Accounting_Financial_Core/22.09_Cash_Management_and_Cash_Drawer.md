# Cash Management and Cash Drawer

## Purpose

Cash is the highest fraud-risk asset in any business.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 implements strict controls over physical cash handling.

---

## Cash Locations

The ERP can manage multiple cash containers:

- Main Cash Vault
- POS Cash Drawer
- Petty Cash Box
- Field Operations Cash
- Delivery Collection Cash

Each location maintains its own ledger and balance.

---

---

## Variance Handling

The system requires:

- Explanation of variance
- Manager review
- Approval for adjustment
- Permanent audit records

---

## Security Controls

The system must support:

- Cash opening and closing approval
- Role-based cash permissions
- Transaction history
- Cash count verification
- Surprise cash audits

---

## Accounting Integration

Cash movement automatically updates:

Debit:
Cash Asset

Credit:
Revenue, Receivable, or other related accounts

or vice versa depending on transaction type.
