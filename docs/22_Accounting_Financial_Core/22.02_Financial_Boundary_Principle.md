# Financial Boundary Principle

## The ERP Records Financial Events, Not Physical Money

PickUrVeggie ERP does not transfer money.

It records transactions and maintains financial history.

---

## Example: Cash Payment

Actual world:

Customer gives ₱2,000 cash.

ERP record:

Cash Asset + ₱2,000

Sales Revenue + ₱2,000

---

## Example: GCash Payment

Actual world:

Customer sends money through GCash.

ERP record:

GCash Wallet Asset increases.

Sales Revenue increases.

---

## Example: Bank Transfer

Actual world:

Customer deposits money into the company bank account.

ERP records:

Bank Asset increases.

Accounts Receivable decreases.

---

## Reconciliation Principle

ERP balances must periodically be compared with real-world balances.

Examples:

Cash Count

Actual:
₱10,500

ERP:
₱10,700

Difference:
₱200 shortage

This difference must be investigated and documented.

---

## Separation of Duties

To prevent fraud:

A person who records transactions should not automatically approve adjustments.

Examples of controlled actions:

- Large expense approvals
- Inventory adjustments
- Payroll approvals
- Financial corrections
- Account write-offs

---

## Audit Requirement

Financial records must preserve:

- Who created the transaction
- Who approved it
- Date and time
- Original values
- Modified values
- Reason for changes

No financial history should disappear.
