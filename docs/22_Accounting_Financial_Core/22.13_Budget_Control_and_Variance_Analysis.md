# Budget Control and Variance Analysis

## Purpose

Budgets allow the farm to compare planned spending against actual results.

The ERP does not only show what happened.

It warns management when spending exceeds expectations.

---

## Budget Levels

Budgets may be created by:

- Company
- Farm location
- Department
- Production block
- Crop cycle
- Project

---

---

## Variance Alerts

The ERP can notify management when:

- Spending exceeds budget
- A category exceeds its limit
- A crop becomes unprofitable
- Unexpected costs appear

---

## Budget Approval

Budget creation requires:

- Creator
- Approval authority
- Effective period
- Version history

---

## Historical Budget Protection

Old budgets are never overwritten.

Example:

2026 Eggplant Budget V1

Remains available even after:

2026 Eggplant Budget V2

is created.

---

## Management Benefits

Budget analysis helps answer:

- Which crops are exceeding cost targets?
- Why did production costs increase?
- Which departments require cost control?
- Are actual expenses aligned with plans?
