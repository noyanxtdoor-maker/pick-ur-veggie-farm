# Crop Production Cost Accounting

## Purpose

Agriculture requires specialized accounting because crops consume resources over time before generating revenue.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 tracks the complete cost journey from seed to sale.

---

## Direct Production Costs

The ERP tracks direct costs such as:

- Seeds
- Seedlings
- Fertilizers
- Nutrients
- Growing media
- Packaging
- Crop protection products
- Direct labor
- Water
- Electricity


---

## Indirect Cost Allocation

Shared costs may be distributed using allocation drivers.

Examples:

Electricity:

Based on:
- Pump runtime
- Flow meter readings
- Area ratio


Labor:

Based on:
- Time tickets
- Assigned tasks
- Crop block workload


Equipment:

Based on:
- Operating hours
- Usage records


---

## Crop Cost Centers

Each production area may become a separate cost center.

Examples:

- DFG Eggplant Tunnel A
- Tomato Greenhouse B
- NFT Lettuce Zone C


This allows management to compare profitability.
