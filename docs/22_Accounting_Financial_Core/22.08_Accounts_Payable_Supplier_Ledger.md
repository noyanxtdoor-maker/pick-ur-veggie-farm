# Accounts Payable Supplier Ledger

## Purpose

Accounts Payable (AP) tracks all money that the farm owes to suppliers, contractors, service providers, and other creditors.

The purpose is to ensure that all obligations are properly recorded, monitored, and paid on time.

---

## Supplier Financial Profile

Every supplier has a financial ledger containing:

- Supplier name
- Contact information
- Payment terms
- Credit limit (if applicable)
- Tax information
- Purchase history
- Outstanding balances
- Payment history

## Accounts Payable Workflow

Purchase Order

        ↓

Goods Receiving

        ↓

Supplier Invoice

        ↓

Accounts Payable Created

        ↓

Payment Approval

        ↓

Payment Release

        ↓

Supplier Balance Updated

---

## Payment Terms Management

The system supports:

- Cash on delivery (COD)
- 7-day terms
- 15-day terms
- 30-day terms
- 60-day terms
- Custom payment agreements

---

## Supplier Aging Analysis

Unpaid balances are categorized:

Current:
0–30 days

Warning:
31–60 days

Overdue:
61–90 days

Critical:
90+ days

---

---

## Purchase Returns

If defective goods are returned:

Example:

Original Purchase:
₱20,000 fertilizer

Returned Materials:
₱5,000

The ERP creates:

Supplier Credit Note

or

Reduction of Accounts Payable

The complete financial history remains intact.

---

## Supplier Payment Controls

The system prevents:

- Paying the same invoice twice
- Payments beyond the invoice balance
- Payments without approval
- Unauthorized supplier changes

---

## Integration with Purchasing

Purchase Order

        ↓

Inventory Receiving

        ↓

Accounts Payable

        ↓

Cash/Bank/Wallet Payment

        ↓

General Ledger
