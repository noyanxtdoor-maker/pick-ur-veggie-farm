# Multi-Company and Consolidation

## Purpose

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 is designed as a multi-tenant enterprise platform.

A single installation may support multiple companies while keeping their data isolated.

Examples:

Company A:
SmartIsland AgriTech Farm

Company B:
Future Farm Partnership

Each company has independent:

- Users
- Inventory
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Financial records
- Production data
- Reports

---

## Data Isolation Principle

Users only access companies where they have permission.

Example:

Juan is an operator for Company A.

He cannot see:

- Company B sales
- Company B payroll
- Company B financial reports

---

## Shared User Accounts

One person may belong to multiple companies.

Example:

Owner Account

Company A:
Owner

Company B:
Consultant

The ERP loads permissions based on the active company workspace.

---

## Consolidated Reporting

Organizations with multiple companies may generate:

- Consolidated sales reports
- Combined financial statements
- Cross-company performance comparisons

---

## Intercompany Transactions

The ERP supports transactions between related companies.

Examples:

Company A sells seedlings to Company B.

Company A records:

Sales Revenue

Company B records:

Inventory Purchase

Both sides maintain independent accounting records.

---

## Security Requirements

Multi-company systems require:

- Strict database isolation
- Role-based permissions
- Company-specific audit logs
- Independent accounting periods

---

## Enterprise Scalability

This architecture allows PickUrVeggie ERP V3 to grow from:

Single Farm

↓

Multiple Farms

↓

Multiple Companies

↓

Agricultural Group Enterprise
