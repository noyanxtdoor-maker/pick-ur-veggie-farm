# Payroll Accounting Integration

## Purpose

Payroll is not only an HR process.

In PickUrVeggie ERP V3, payroll directly affects:

- Labor expenses
- Crop production costs
- Employee liabilities
- Cash and bank balances
- Profitability analysis

---

## Payroll Financial Lifecycle

Employee Attendance

        ↓

Approved Timesheets

        ↓

Payroll Calculation

        ↓

Payroll Approval

        ↓

Accounting Journal Creation

        ↓

Salary Payment

        ↓

Financial Reconciliation

---

## Payroll Expense Classification

Labor costs may be classified based on the activity performed.

Examples:

### Direct Production Labor

Examples:

- Harvesting
- Transplanting
- Fertigation
- Pruning
- Packaging

Accounting Treatment:

Assigned directly to:

- Crop Work-In-Progress
- Biological Asset
- Harvest Inventory

depending on the production stage.

---

### Indirect Farm Labor

Examples:

- Farm supervisor
- Security
- Maintenance personnel

Accounting Treatment:

Recorded as operating expense or allocated using approved cost allocation methods.

---

### Administrative Labor

Examples:

- Accounting staff
- Office workers
- Sales personnel

Accounting Treatment:

General administrative expense.

---

## Cash Advance Integration

Employee receives:

₱5,000 cash advance


Initial Entry:


Debit:

Employee Advance Receivable    ₱5,000


Credit:

Cash                            ₱5,000


During payroll:


Debit:

Salary Payable                  ₱5,000


Credit:

Employee Advance Receivable     ₱5,000


---

## Payroll Controls

The ERP prevents:

- Paying unapproved payroll
- Duplicate salary payments
- Unauthorized salary adjustments
- Deleting payroll history

---

## Management Benefits

The owner can analyze:

- Labor cost per crop
- Labor cost per kilogram harvested
- Department payroll costs
- Overtime trends
- Workforce efficiency
