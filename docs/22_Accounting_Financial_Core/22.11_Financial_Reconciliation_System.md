# Financial Reconciliation System

## Purpose

Reconciliation ensures that ERP records match real-world financial balances.

---

## Types of Reconciliation

The ERP supports:

- Cash reconciliation
- Bank reconciliation
- GCash reconciliation
- Maya reconciliation
- Accounts receivable reconciliation
- Accounts payable reconciliation
- Inventory valuation reconciliation

---

## Cash Reconciliation Example

ERP Balance:
₱10,000

Physical Cash Count:
₱9,700

Difference:
₱300 shortage

Action Required:

- Investigate the discrepancy
- Document findings
- Obtain approval before adjustments

---

## Bank Reconciliation Example

ERP Bank Balance:
₱500,000

Bank Statement:
₱495,000

Possible causes:

- Outstanding checks
- Deposits in transit
- Bank charges
- Recording errors

---

## Reconciliation Workflow

System Balance

        ↓

External Verification

        ↓

Identify Differences

        ↓

Create Adjustment Proposal

        ↓

Management Approval

        ↓

Post Adjustment Entry

---

## Adjustment Controls

Adjustments require:

- Explanation
- Supporting documents
- Approval authority
- Audit logs

---

## Period Closing Requirement

Financial periods cannot be closed until critical reconciliation procedures are completed.

Examples:

- Cash verified
- Bank accounts reconciled
- Major inventory differences investigated
- Significant customer or supplier disputes reviewed

---

## Fraud Prevention

The reconciliation system helps detect:

- Missing cash
- Duplicate payments
- Unauthorized transactions
- Recording mistakes
- Inventory irregularities

---

## ERP Integrity Principle

A beautiful financial report is meaningless if the underlying balances are wrong.

Reconciliation protects the reliability and trustworthiness of all financial statements.
