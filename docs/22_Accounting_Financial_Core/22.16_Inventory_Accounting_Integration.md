# Inventory Accounting Integration

## Purpose

Inventory is not merely a quantity tracker.

In PickUrVeggie ERP V3, every inventory movement has both:

- Physical impact
- Financial impact

The inventory module and accounting module must always remain synchronized.

---

## Inventory Financial Lifecycle

Purchase

        ↓

Raw Material Inventory Asset

        ↓

Consumption

        ↓

Crop Work-In-Progress Cost

        ↓

Biological Asset

        ↓

Harvest Inventory

        ↓

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

---

## Inventory Movement Financial Rules


### Purchase of Materials

Example:

Purchase 10 bags of Calcium Nitrate for ₱30,000.

Accounting Entry:

Debit:
Fertilizer Inventory Asset      ₱30,000

Credit:
Cash or Accounts Payable        ₱30,000


---

### Material Consumption

Example:

Use 20 kg Calcium Nitrate for Eggplant Tunnel A.

Accounting Entry:

Debit:
Crop WIP - Eggplant Tunnel A    ₱1,200

Credit:
Fertilizer Inventory            ₱1,200


---

### Transfer Between Locations

Example:

Transfer fertilizer from Main Warehouse to Tunnel A.

Physical inventory changes location.

Accounting impact:

None.

The ownership of the asset remains the same.

---

### Inventory Adjustment

Examples:

- Count shortage
- Damage
- Theft
- Expired materials

The ERP requires:

- Reason code
- Evidence
- Approval
- Audit record


Example:

Spoilage:

Debit:
Inventory Loss Expense          ₱500

Credit:
Inventory Asset                 ₱500


---

## Inventory Valuation Principles

The system maintains:

- Quantity on hand
- Reserved quantity
- Available quantity
- Batch cost
- Purchase history
- Expiration dates


---

## Anti-Fraud Controls

The ERP prevents:

- Negative inventory balances
- Unapproved stock adjustments
- Deletion of historical movements
- Backdated inventory changes after period closing


---

## Accounting Integrity Rule

The total inventory value in accounting must always equal the value calculated by the inventory ledger.

Differences must trigger reconciliation and investigation.
