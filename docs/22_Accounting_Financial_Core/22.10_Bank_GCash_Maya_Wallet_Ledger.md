# Bank, GCash, and Digital Wallet Ledger

## Purpose

Modern farms receive and make payments through multiple channels.

The ERP maintains separate ledgers for each financial account.

---

## Supported Accounts

Examples:

Bank Accounts:
- BDO
- BPI
- LandBank
- Other banks

Digital Wallets:
- GCash
- Maya
- Other e-wallet services

---

## Important Principle

The ERP does NOT hold money.

It records the financial movement of money that occurs in real life.

---

## Account Ledger Example

GCash Business Wallet

Opening Balance:
₱25,000


Customer Payment:
+ ₱10,000


Supplier Payment:
- ₱5,000


Closing Balance:
₱30,000

---

## Transaction Records

Each entry contains:

- Date and time
- Amount
- Transaction type
- Reference number
- Related customer or supplier
- User who recorded the transaction
- Notes

---

## Transfers Between Accounts

Example:

Transfer ₱20,000

From:
GCash

To:
BPI Bank

ERP Entry:

Decrease GCash Balance

Increase Bank Balance

No income or expense is created.

---

## Payment Gateway Separation

The ERP may integrate with payment services in the future.

However:

Payment processing happens outside the ERP.

The ERP only records the resulting financial transaction.

---

## Security Features

- Separate permissions per financial account
- Approval for large transfers
- Complete transaction history
- Fraud monitoring reports
- Audit logs
