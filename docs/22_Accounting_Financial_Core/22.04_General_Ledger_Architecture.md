# General Ledger Architecture

## Purpose

The General Ledger (GL) is the central financial database of PickUrVeggie ERP V3.

All financial transactions from every ERP module ultimately flow into the General Ledger.

The GL serves as the single source of financial truth.

---

## Modules Connected to the General Ledger

The GL receives transactions from:

- Purchasing
- Inventory
- Crop Production
- Biological Assets
- Harvest Management
- Sales & POS
- Accounts Receivable
- Accounts Payable
- Payroll
- Equipment & Fixed Assets
- Cash Management

---

## General Ledger Structure

Each GL entry contains:

- Transaction ID
- Journal Entry Number
- Date and Time
- Accounting Period
- Source Module
- Source Document Reference
- Debit Account
- Credit Account
- Amount
- Currency
- Description
- Created By
- Approved By
- Audit Trail Reference

## General Ledger Rules

1. Every entry must have an identifiable source.

No "mystery" financial transactions are allowed.

---

2. The total debits must always equal total credits.

The ledger must always remain balanced.

---

3. Approved entries cannot be deleted.

Corrections require adjustment entries.

---

4. Every transaction maintains historical records.

The ERP records:

- Original values
- Modified values
- User responsible
- Date and time
- Reason for adjustment

---

## Financial Integrity Principle

The General Ledger is read-only after approval.

Operational users cannot directly modify accounting history.

This prevents fraud, accidental errors, and manipulation of financial reports.
