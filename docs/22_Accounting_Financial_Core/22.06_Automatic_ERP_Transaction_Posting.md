# Automatic ERP Transaction Posting

## Philosophy

The best ERP systems do not rely on users manually entering accounting records.

Operational activities automatically create financial transactions.

---

## Purchase Workflow

Purchase Order Approved

        ↓

Goods Received

        ↓

Inventory Updated

        ↓

Automatic Journal Creation

Example:

Debit:
Inventory Asset

Credit:
Supplier Payable

---

## Inventory Consumption Workflow

Fertilizer used in Tunnel A

        ↓

Inventory decreases

        ↓

Crop Production Cost increases

        ↓

Journal automatically generated

---

## Harvest Workflow

Harvest completed

        ↓

Crop value calculated

        ↓

Biological Asset reduced

        ↓

Finished Goods Inventory increased

---

## Sales Workflow

Customer purchase completed

        ↓

Sales invoice generated

        ↓

Revenue recognized

        ↓

Inventory reduced

        ↓

COGS recognized

---

## Payroll Workflow

Payroll approved

        ↓

Labor Expense recorded

        ↓

Salary Payable created

---

## Equipment Purchase Workflow

Equipment purchased

        ↓

System evaluates capitalization policy

        ↓

Either:

Fixed Asset

or

Operating Expense

---

## Posting Controls

Automatic postings must include:

- Source transaction
- Transaction date
- Responsible user
- Company
- Location
- Supporting documents

---

## Error Handling

If posting fails:

The system:

- Prevents incomplete financial records
- Creates an error alert
- Logs the failure
- Requires authorized resolution

Financial records must never be partially posted.
