# Accounting Period Closing

## Purpose

Accounting periods prevent historical financial records from being continuously changed.

Example periods:

- Daily closing
- Weekly closing
- Monthly closing
- Quarterly closing
- Annual closing

---

## Period Closing Workflow

Complete operational transactions

        ↓

Complete reconciliations

        ↓

Review financial statements

        ↓

Management approval

        ↓

Lock accounting period

---

## Closing Checklist

Before a period can be closed, the system verifies:

### Sales

- All invoices posted
- Returns processed
- Customer balances reviewed

### Purchasing

- Supplier invoices recorded
- Purchase returns completed

### Inventory

- Stock counts completed
- Major variances investigated

### Payroll

- Payroll approved
- Salary liabilities reviewed

### Cash and Banking

- Cash reconciled
- Bank reconciled
- Digital wallet balances verified

---

## Closed Period Protection

Example:

June 2026:
CLOSED

Users cannot:

- Add new transactions
- Edit transactions
- Delete transactions

inside June 2026.

---

## Period Reopening

Only authorized users may reopen periods.

Requirements:

- Reason for reopening
- Approval authority
- Complete audit record

---

## Year-End Closing

The system supports:

- Final financial reports
- Profit and loss closing
- Retained earnings updates
- New fiscal year creation

---

## Accounting Integrity Rule

Financial reports should be reproducible.

A report generated today for June 2026 should produce the same result five years later.
