# Expense Request and Approval Workflow

## Purpose

Not every expense should immediately become a payment.

PickUrVeggie ERP V3 separates:

- Expense requests
- Approval
- Purchasing
- Payment
- Accounting recognition

This creates financial control and reduces fraud.

---

## Expense Request Sources

Requests may originate from:

- Farm workers
- Operators
- Managers
- Administrators
- Owners

Examples:

- Emergency fertilizer purchase
- Equipment repair
- Vehicle fuel
- Office supplies
- Farm tools
- Delivery expenses

---

## Expense Request Workflow

Employee Creates Request

        ↓

Supervisor Review

        ↓

Budget Availability Check

        ↓

Management Approval

        ↓

Purchase or Expense Authorization

        ↓

Actual Payment

        ↓

Accounting Entry Generated

---

## Expense Request Information

Every request records:

- Request ID
- Request date
- Requestor
- Department
- Project or crop block
- Expense category
- Estimated amount
- Business justification
- Priority level
- Attachments

---

## Approval Levels

Small expenses:

Example:
₱500 farm supplies

May require supervisor approval only.

---

Medium expenses:

Example:
₱5,000 equipment repair

May require manager approval.

---

Large expenses:

Example:
₱100,000 machinery purchase

May require owner or executive approval.

---

Approval thresholds are configurable.

---

## Emergency Expenses

The ERP supports emergency approvals.

Example:

Irrigation pump fails during extreme heat.

Operator submits urgent repair request.

The system:

- Marks it as emergency
- Sends immediate alerts
- Applies special approval rules

---

## Expense Verification

Before payment, the ERP can require:

- Supplier invoice
- Receipt
- Photo evidence
- Delivery confirmation
- Manager acceptance

---

## Fraud Prevention

The system prevents:

- Duplicate expense claims
- Unauthorized purchases
- Missing documentation
- Payments without approval

All actions remain in the audit trail.
